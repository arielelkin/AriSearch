# Fresh review: ternary species propagation and opposite-A palette obstruction

Date: 2026-10-12. Verdict: VALID within the explicit generic angle-star domain.

Reviewed proofs:
[ternary stars/balance](../lemmas/two-hexagon-ternary-angle-star-balance.md) and
[opposite-A palette theorem](../lemmas/two-hexagon-ternary-opposite-a-palette-obstruction.md).
These statements restrict actual polygons through the previously reviewed
hexagon defect-free limit, not an assumed edge-to-edge source tiling.

The identity derivation is exhaustive. With n B angles in a triple,
substitution gives a_i-2b_i=1-n. The nonnegative integer cases are exactly
AAA one of each, AAB two equal A colours with the associated B colour,
and BBB one of each. No ABB identity exists. Generic explicitly removes
all other proper affine star hyperplanes; no conclusion about those omitted
angle specializations is made. The18+8+8 native count and the11 balanced
ternary bracelets are complete by native label choices and dihedral orbits.
The irrational sample follows from rational independence of1,sqrt2,sqrt3.

No ABB means that consecutive neighbors around any B centre have the same
species. A B-B edge consequently makes that entire neighbor cycle B, and
connected cell adjacency propagates the pure B field. In a genuinely mixed
field BBB stars are impossible and the invariant ledger gives N_A>=2N_B,
with pure AAA intensity2N_A-4N_B. PureA/pureB fields give a periodic
monohedral escape by the reviewed unrestricted convex-hexagon result.

The endpoint-palette gate is also valid. Two equal side-length native types
with the same unordered endpoint-colour multiset have matching colours at
one shared endpoint iff they match at the other. For a repeated-colour pair
both agree; for a distinct pair the two possible endpoint bijections agree
at both endpoints or disagree at both. At AAA the two A colours are different,
at AAB equal. Thus each ray at an AAA vertex propagates AAA to its next
vertex, and connected vertex adjacency gives a pure A field. This argument
retains reflection and needs no native orientation uniqueness. A genuinely
mixed palette field therefore has only AAB stars and the reviewed one-B
phase applies.

For A012012, every placed colour profile has period three and its consecutive
three slots use all colours. The central and opposite AA endpoint formulas
in the proof yield equality of third colours absent from
{b_k,b_(k-1)} and{b_k,b_(k+1)}. Adjacent equal B colours would already
prevent an A neighbor. Otherwise this equality forces b_(k-1)=b_(k+1)
for allk, so the B word has period two and uses at most two colours. This
contradicts its actual two copies of each of three distinct colours. This
is an unbounded local contradiction; no finite torus failure is a premise.
The retained24-ring tiny census corroborates it but is not required to trust
the infinite conclusion.

The limit may omit a species even if the original tiling used both. The
statement correctly concludes an admitted periodic escape from that pure
limit, not periodicity or purity of every original partial-contact tiling.
Generic exceptions, other A native words, and A length classes joining
different endpoint-colour multisets remain outside this result. No claim
of full two-convex-polygon impossibility or a viable construction is made.
