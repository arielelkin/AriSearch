# Fresh hostile review: two-pentagon positive-conjugate inflation bound

Date: 2026-10-12. **Verdict: VALID**, conditional on the already reviewed
general six-piece convex-pentagon dissection theorem. Reviewer independent
of the positive-conjugate proof.

The area vector is positive and the replacement matrix is an integral
2-by-2 matrix. A quadratic irrational eigenvalue has degree two, so its
monic minimal polynomial equals the characteristic polynomial. In
particular both algebraic conjugates are eigenvalues and the determinant
is their product. An assumed positive conjugate gives strictly positive
determinant; a rational or higher-degree factor would require a different
argument and is correctly excluded from the hypotheses.

An individual one-child replacement is the potential loophole in a bare
minimum-area argument. The proof handles both possibilities: a child of
the same prototile violates the positive area expansion; a child of the
other prototile gives an off-diagonal unit column and determinant at most
zero. This contradicts determinant positivity even when the two shapes
are similar but differently sized. A zero-child image also cannot have
the parent's positive area. Thus both images really are nontrivial.

The six-piece theorem now applies to both complete pentagon images,
including arbitrary partial contacts and reflected children. The smaller
prototile's image contains at least six positive-area children, each no
smaller than it. Therefore mu>=6. No primitiveness assumption, assumption
that shapes are nonsimilar, or assumption that the area ratio is irrational
is missing. The matrix eigenvalue argument is enough.

The two explicit examples have positive conjugates and mu<6:
phi^2=(3+sqrt5)/2 and (1+sqrt2)^2=3+2sqrt2. The claimed exclusions follow.
They prohibit ordinary two-pentagon substitutions at those linear scales,
not all full-plane tilings, non-stationary hierarchies, or arbitrary source
recompositions. The text correctly separates more species, compound parent
regions and nonpositive conjugates from its conclusion. It also makes no
existence assertion at mu>=6. No formal solver result is used.
