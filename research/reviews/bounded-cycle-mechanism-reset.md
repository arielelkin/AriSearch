## Three substantially different proposed mechanisms

These are proposed next experiments, not results or newly authorized extra
cycles. Each has a concrete first gate and explicit failure boundary.

1. **Construct a tiling with one translation from infinite strips.** The
   target forbids even one nonzero period, while current carrier searches
   mostly seek rank2 finite tori. For a residual fixed ray, construct an exact
   finite-width strip motif whose two ends match under a nonzero translation,
   then retain its full original-native top/bottom interval profiles. Attempt
   a finite cycle of exactly compatible strip profiles covering the plane,
   or a rigorously extendable infinite profile path with a common longitudinal
   translation. Verify all actual cuts, seam endpoints, nonoverlap and strip
   coverage before the extension step. A successful strip construction kills
   aperiodicity even if transverse stacking is not periodic. An incompatible
   bounded strip ansatz does not exclude all tilings; without an extension
   proof stop rather than call a local strip a periodic plane tiling.

2. **Use actual Euclidean cycle transports beyond marginal balance.** On the
   pure/mixed and pure/pure hexagon categories, assign each permitted native
   whole-side adjacency its exact rigid isometry, retaining both reflections
   and native endpoint roles. Reconstruct a complete central tile and its
   second corona; compare compositions along paths ending at the same actual
   occurrence. Rotation and translation must agree exactly. A nonidentity
   closed transport or an overlap of forced occurrences can obstruct every
   continuation of a native case, something frequency balance cannot see.
   First gate: a small independently checkable cycle inconsistency covering
   all geometric alternatives for one residual native star. Promotion to a
   family theorem requires exhausting actual alternatives and exceptional
   metric loci. The monohexagon two-corona comparison in Zhu–Wang–Yan is a
   method reference, not an imported result for mixed-species tilings.

3. **Direct inverse design of a recognizable expansive hierarchy.** Explore
   exact acute pentagons with T-junctions outside the current Cairo/pentagrid
   angle templates. Start from a chosen primitive two-species substitution
   matrix and solve exact boundary decompositions and convex support
   inequalities for two original shapes and an expansive geometric map.
   First gate: one exact dissection and a locally forced unique level1
   grouping that survives reflections and unintended partial contacts,
   together with a rigorous infinite tiling. A complete construction must
   prove every admitted tiling desubstitutes recognizably at all levels;
   shrinking any hypothetical period under arbitrarily many desubstitutions
   would then contradict the fixed positive minimum tile inradius. An
   irrational frequency alone excludes neither rank1 periods nor unintended
   assemblies. This is direct geometry design, not reopening the blocked
   fixed-source convex-fusion compiler without changing its assumptions.
