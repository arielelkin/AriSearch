# Hostile review: pentagon curvature and syndeticity

**Artifact reviewed:** [pentagon-curvature.md](../lemmas/pentagon-curvature.md).

**Date:** 2026-10-02
**Verdict:** **Valid.**

The exact identity was checked by substituting
\(D=(2E-q)-3(V-V_b)\),
\(\delta=2E-B-S\), and \(V-E+N=1-H\).  This gives

\[
\delta+2D=6N-S+6V_b-2q-B-6+6H.
\]

The existing uniform boundary estimates make the last five terms \(O(R)\).
Subdivision slack and excess valence are nonnegative and cover the intended
contact defects.

Pentagon syndeticity for a pentagon paired with an \(n\)-gon, \(n\ge7\), was
checked by contradiction using recentered pentagon-free disks and local
compactness.  The limiting monohedral \(n\)-gon tiling contradicts the global
at-least-seven-side obstruction.  For \(n=6\), the same argument is valid as
a necessary condition for aperiodicity after invoking the named unrestricted
convex-hexagon classification.  A separated grid with spacing larger than
twice the disk radius plus maximum tile diameter converts syndeticity into a
uniform quadratic lower bound on pentagon counts.

The positive-density defect conclusions follow by substituting those bounds
into the exact identity.  They are necessary conditions, not constructions.
