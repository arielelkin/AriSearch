# Independent review: alternating A with six distinct side lengths

Reviewed canonical plural-path [lemma](../lemmas/two-hexagon-alternating-distinct-a-sides-obstruction.md).
Verdict: valid, conditional only on its cited reviewed defect-free reduction.

Across an AA whole seam distinct lengths force the identical native side
index, independent of either tile's rotation/reflection. Because its endpoint
colours differ, same-colour AAB stars force the same native corner at each
physical endpoint. Reflection in the common supporting line is the unique
isometry putting the second congruent A on the opposite side while fixing
both endpoints. The other native side at that endpoint is consequently the
same index on the two A copies and has the same actual length.

Those other sides are exactly the two AB rays bounding that B corner.
Whole-side matching therefore makes the two incident native B lengths
equal. Every B corner has this property, giving an equilateral actual B.
Each A has three AB neighbours in the one-B phase, so three distinct native
A sides would have to equal that same B length. This is contradictory.

No global parity propagation, connected A graph, commutation of reflections
or carrier construction is needed. The corresponding paragraph in the
initial text is extraneous to the conclusion; removing it leaves the proof
intact. Coincident A lengths are correctly outside scope. The local argument
rules out a defect-free whole-side field; the reviewed reduction is what
extends the conclusion to arbitrary original partial-contact tilings.
No gap found in the short local proof.
