## Exact reduction and seam conventions

[The theory](../lemmas/two-hexagon-one-b-ring-spatial-reduction.md) applies to arbitrary admitted 2A+1B multisets and side equivalences, including reflected placement symbols. The shared-edge comparison of the two adjacent elementary triangles proves the B indicator is invariant under all Delta_k. Delta_0=(1,1), Delta_1=(-1,2) generate an index-three lattice. Each elementary triangle meets its three cosets once; exactly one coset must therefore be B. This is a species scaffold, not a period of the full labelled configuration.

The reviewer independently derived the six central ring star incidences from translated canonical triangles in [the ring checker](check_two_hexagon_balanced_rank_five_ring.py). The slots really are k,k+2,k+4, even when the canonical anchor is an A neighbour. For two B centers separated by Delta_k, coordinate subtraction places the shared A cells at second-center slots k+4 and k+3. Full placement equality at those two positions is the correct pair relation. No reverse/reflection shift is missing.

The metric seam formulas were separately checked by enumerating adjacent lattice cells of the seven-cell patch and recovering native sides from their two corner endpoints. A B/A seam in direction D_k compares side slots k−1 and k+2. The A/A seam between D_k,D_(k+1) compares k+1,k+4. For a reflected placement the native side is r−k−1, preserving the required offset. The independent 600-order audit uses this endpoint-derived side map when validating the branching metric closures and torus labels.

The exact lifting claim holds. Every A cell neighbours three B centers; those centers form a triangle in the B lattice. Pair relations equate all three occurrences of its full placement. Conversely every complete star contains one B and is checked in that B's ring. Every B/A seam is in its B ring, and every A/A seam is present in the B rings at its endpoints. There are no additional untested seams. The construction is bijective and preserves translations in the B lattice. Side filtering only restricts states and pair relations, so angle-only deletion certificates remain valid for every metric.

## One-direction functional criterion

Outdegree at most one in one direction, on a finite full state set containing every possible global state, is enough. Every row is bi-infinite. A repetition in its infinite past lies on a directed cycle, and a partial function cannot exit that cycle. Thus every occurring state lies on a cycle, with length at most the alphabet size. The common multiple lcm(1,...,|C|) is a period of **every row simultaneously**, irrespective of transverse branching or predecessor ambiguities outside the globally occurring states. Ring lifting transfers this period to all placement labels, and the reviewed global SFT development gives a genuine geometric periodic escape for any actual polygon pair that tiles.

Neither unique B species nor unique B placement is enough. The function must act on the complete B placement plus six A placements. A quotient by physical symmetry would require its own proof that transitions and incidence data are well-defined. The theory uses full symbols and avoids that issue. The empty-state case is already covered by the spatial exclusion theorem; lcm notation is needed only for a nonempty core.

The converse is absent and should remain absent. Branching after finite pruning does not prove infinite extension or aperiodicity. A periodic point of an angle-only superset also does not prove a periodic escape for arbitrary side lengths. In contrast, if every point of that superset is forced periodic by the functional theorem, every actual matched configuration inherits that period. The theory's metric torus gate is the correct separate existential witness mechanism for branching cases.

## Two-basis bound and wording repair

When both basis relations are functional, the globally occurring states lie on cycles for each function. Their cyclic predecessors are unique even if the unrestricted relation has extra incoming tails. The two translations commute on the occurring orbit, so a return of the central state in either direction propagates to every lattice site, including negative basis directions. This justifies periods n_0*Delta_0,n_1*Delta_1 with each n_i at most |C|.

The subgroup generated by these two periods has index 3*n_0*n_1 in the original cell lattice. The **full** placement period lattice may include additional diagonal periods and therefore has index **at most** this value, not necessarily equal to it. The stated useful upper bound 3*|C|² is valid. The reviewer requested this equality-to-upper-bound wording repair, and the retained lemma now states the corrected subgroup index and full-lattice upper bound. It changes no finite-bound conclusion.

## Concrete failure of the unique-star shortcut

The exact closed rank-five type

    {(0,1,6),(0,2,7),(1,2,8),(3,3,9),(4,4,10),(5,5,11)}

has one star for each native B corner, positive weights 1/3 and species weights 2/3,1/3. Nonetheless B placement (0,+1) admits two different A rings:

    [8,3,3,8,10,6]
    [8,11,11,8,10,6].

A state indices 0..5 mean (r,+1), and 6..11 mean (r,−1), in increasing r. [The exact witness checker](check_two_hexagon_unique_B_star_multiring.py) verifies rational closure, rank, balance, both rings and a rational generic angle vector with all twelve angles distinct and exactly these six stars. The changed A states carry different actual angles at a common slot; this is not just redundant labels at equal angles. [The retained witness](two-hexagon-unique-B-star-multiring-witness.json) makes the missing local determinism concrete.

This is a local angle-type counterexample to the inference from unique B stars to unique full rings. It does not assert a simultaneous metric or infinite polygon realization, and it does not contradict the functional theorem.
