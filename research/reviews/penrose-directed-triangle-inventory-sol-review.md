The hypothesis permits arbitrary integral M; the converse mentioning nonnegative M is a sufficient specialization, not the full converse under that hypothesis. 3. **Spectral argument passes.** Direct exact checks give SB=BT and SE=ER, where E has columns (1,-1,0,0) and (0,0,1,-1), and R=[[0,-1],[1,1]]. The quadratic characteristic factors are irreducible over Q and coprime. A nonzero rank-one rational invariant image is impossible. A two-dimensional invariant rational image splits under the rational spectral projectors; each nonzero component has dimension two, so it is exactly U or V. V intersects the nonnegative orthant only at zero. Hence every column balances both chiralities. This proof needs neither Perron positivity of M nor bounded enumeration.

4. **Directional counterexample passes.** Producer replay gives all four stated exact vectors, with 1/phi represented as -w³+w². Independently checked all four kite turns are positive in the chosen real embedding and opposite-vertex sums differ, proving strict convexity and failure of central symmetry. The balanced A pair has the stated nonzero directional vector. Thus chirality balance alone cannot force central symmetry. Independent proper rotations preserve ordered chirality and change directional components; reflections exchange the signs. A reflection-permitting output model requires extra state or explicit reflection equivariance, as the lemma says.

5. **Convex corner and minimal-output gates pass with their assumptions.** In the common frame, exterior angle contributions are (5-k)*36 degrees; their sum gives 4n1+3n2+2n3+n4=10.
