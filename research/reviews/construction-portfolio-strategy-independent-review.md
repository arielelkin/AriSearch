# Independent review of the construction portfolio strategy

Date: 2026-10-13. Reviewer: frontier consolidation agent. Verdict: **VALID as
a research strategy**, subject to the explicit proof gates below. This is not
an endorsement of an existing candidate or an assertion of eventual success.

Reviewed [strategy](../searches/construction-portfolio-strategy.md) against
the original problem and the retained H/D incidence, source-by-source area,

## Assumptions and attacks

The strategy correctly requires an exact infinite covering before an extensive
congruence/geometry campaign and separately demands forcing for every admitted
tilings, including reflections, partial contacts, species omission and a single
translation. Its level-n marker criterion is sufficient: any nonzero period
moves a marker to a distinct marker at that same fixed distance, contradicting
the diverging separation bound. Nonempty marker sets and equivariance must be
proved in every admitted tiling, as the strategy explicitly requires.

Track A genuinely changes the H/D incidence theorem only if a new third face,
branching incidence or face reassignment is exhibited. Merely moving corners
or filling the same notches stays excluded. Fractional source pieces and tiles
crossing source boundaries can change the separate parity and source-by-source
area hypotheses; this does not imply that no replacement invariant exists.
The strategy correctly records that distinction. Complete finite-neighbourhood
coverage must be justified at the actual surgery radius; a list of vertex
stars or a large drawn patch is insufficient.

Track B is explicitly conditional on producing an actual parent/contact scheme.
It does not silently designate a speculative hierarchy a live candidate.
Nonconvex parent patches fall outside the exact two-whole-pentagon substitution
theorem, but the strategy retains area/count and recognition obligations. The
positive-conjugate examples phi and 1+sqrt(2) are correctly excluded only for
ordinary substitutions whose two parents are the pentagons themselves.

## Two practical clarifications sent to the author

1. The decoder must land in the precise source class covered by its proven
   aperiodicity theorem, including parameter/genericity and matching hypotheses.
   Reconstructing a finite local atlas alone need not establish membership in
   that class. If decoding has choices, prove uniqueness or an intrinsic
   translation-equivariant selection (or another rigorous period-transfer
   argument); existence of one preferred source reconstruction is insufficient.
2. Before executing the next cycle, predeclare a finite number of parent/surgery
   schemes and a concrete stopping artifact per scheme. Allocation percentages
   alone cannot prevent endless topology editing. This is an execution condition,
   not a request for more search during the current strategy-only assignment.

No retained theorem's obstruction scope is improperly enlarged. No blind bound
increase or automatic reopening of a closed enumeration is proposed. The two
clarifications strengthen implementation of already stated gates and do not
invalidate the strategic recommendation. No external literature claim was
independently rechecked in this review; primary-source novelty remains a gate.
