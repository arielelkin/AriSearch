# Independent hostile review: Sugimoto concave-pair convex exchange gate

Reviewed 2026-10-19. Target: `research/literature/sugimoto-concave-pair-convex-exchange-gate-2026-10-19.md`.

Verdict: **pass, with two wording clarifications; no mathematical blocker found in the stated gate.** No candidate pair or nonexistence theorem follows.

- **Actual plane tilings, not just patterned correspondence.** [Sections 2.1–2.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.09603v1#S2) explicitly transfer the H7/H8 substitution to AH/BH and AT/BT and identify its infinite limits as two-type plane tilings. Erasing the internal pentagon designs leaves genuine unmarked concave prototile tilings. “Plane-source mechanism” is correct but unnecessarily weaker than this source claim. It does not imply every unmarked tiling follows that hierarchy.
- **Aperiodicity remains open.** [Section 5.3](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.09603v1#S5.SS3) leaves other tilings of AH/BH and AT/BT unknown. Section 5.2 supplies periodic AH/AT and BH/BT tilings; these do not settle the first two pairs. Reflections are allowed in the paper’s unrestricted model. The gate correctly keeps existence separate from unrestricted forcing.
- **Archive inventory confirmed.** The inspected extracted v1 package `/tmp/arisearch-sugimoto2609` contains 57 PNGs, one TeX file, and `00README.json` (so “README” means a JSON metadata file). No coordinate or generator code appears. This is an inventory fact, not an obstruction to constructing an exchange.
- **Pentagon reconstruction confirmed exactly.** With the stated E,A,B,C,D coordinates, squared edge lengths are `(1/3,1/3,1,1,1)`. Consecutive edge cross products are `(sqrt(3)/6,sqrt(3)/6,sqrt(3)/2,sqrt(3)/2,sqrt(3)/3)`, all positive; the pentagon is simple and strictly convex. Its E,A,B,C,D interior angles are `(90,120,150,60,120)` degrees, exactly matching [Figure 1.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.09603v1/Fig01-04_AMT.png), with the scale fixed by BC=1. The paper identifies this pentagon as both Type 1 and Type 5 and supplies a periodic representative carrier in Figure 1.1. The native-cell repartition therefore cannot yield an aperiodic two-shape set: it gives one class, and retaining it beside a second shape still permits its periodic monohedral tiling under the stated omission convention.
- **Mystic claim confirmed, but distinguish region from ambient cluster.** [Section 4 and Figure 4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.09603v1#S4) reflect a Mystic region across its line of symmetry; its outline visibly has reflex vertices. Figure 4.5 extends the replacement to hat/turtle patches and preserves the ambient S9 outline. Both the local region and the ambient cluster are nonconvex. The outputs are still concave hat/turtle tiles; the paper does not provide a two-convex repartition. Prefer “preserves the nonconvex Mystic-region boundary (and hence the surrounding cluster boundary)” to remove the present ambiguity.
- **Scope of the stop condition is sound.** No source-specified, globally compatible two-convex exchange with exact endpoints has been identified. That is a missing construction datum for this approach, not proof that no seam exchange or dual-cell construction exists. Exact endpoints and a finite incidence topology are the proposed project gate, not a theorem that every successful approach must use this representation. The later decoder requirement remains an additional proof obligation even if a two-class plane repartition is found.
