# Corrected interleaved hexagon geometry and spatial gate

Verdict: VALID for I/I and for the corrected geometric-plus-spatial I/C argument. I/ALT was outside this review; its subsequent [all-metric closure](../lemmas/two-hexagon-interleaved-alternating-all-metric-escape.md) is separately reviewed.

The first hostile check found a substantive error in the original B=C direction list: it omitted the change to an s-angle exterior turn at native corner 3. Direct accumulation for C=000111 gives theta multipliers `0,2,4,2,0,-2`, whereas the withdrawn calculation used `0,2,4,4,2,0`. The former local-only I/C proof and certificate are withdrawn. The separately retained withdrawn certificate is not a dependency of this review.

The independent checker `check_two_hexagon_interleaved_symbolic_closure.py` derives all three direction lists directly by multiplying successive exterior-turn rotations. It imports no symbolic producer. It reconstructs each closure matrix column by summing the independently derived direction components assigned to that length class, and compares every retained matrix. It independently computes every maximal-rank minor determinant and their exact numerator gcd, verifies all admitted real rank-drop roots, and checks that matrix denominators have no admitted poles.

For generic one-dimensional kernels, it independently isolates all rank-change roots and all roots/poles of the rational basis coordinates. Exact kernels at every partition point and exact midpoint signs on every complementary interval match the certificate. This is a complete sign partition: rank and nonzero rational coordinate signs cannot change elsewhere. Positive kernels are retained rather than rejected. I/I has 105 closures, with 12,240 native rings excluded by full rank and 48 by incompatible coordinate signs. All 12,288 rings are therefore locally impossible at every admitted angle.

Corrected I/C has 67 closures. Full-rank arguments exclude 2,688 rings; exact rank/sign partitions exclude another 276. The remaining special five-class closure has generic nullity two, not one. Its rank-drop gcd `t^3-t` has no admitted roots. In every vector of its two-dimensional kernel, coordinate 2 equals coordinate 1 times `(t^2+1)/(2(t^2-1))`, using zero-based indices 1 and 2. The checker verifies this identity on every generic basis vector and checks any admitted normalization poles against the exact kernel. The ratio is strictly negative throughout `|t|<2-sqrt(3)<1`; these two coordinates cannot both be positive. This excludes twelve additional native rings. Constant rank also extends the identity across a removable normalization pole; no pole of a chosen basis is treated as a polygon singularity.

The 96 locally unexcluded native I/C rings are retained simultaneously, independently of which angles support them. This union is a sound superset for every fixed admitted angle. The checker independently enumerates their native B and six-A placements from canonical triangles, reconstructs their twelve seam classes using seven-cell coordinates, and reproduces the entire compatibility relation by shared A-cell positions. Every ring lacks a necessary triangle support in the initial superset. One deletion round therefore removes all 96 states. This is a spatial obstruction after metric restrictions, not an inference from absence of a bounded periodic solution.

The retained full check covers 32 exact exceptional points and 66 sign intervals, plus the complete 96-state transport. Both cases exclude an admitted trivalent native field. The reviewed defect-free hexagon limit transfers that conclusion to original tilings with reflections, partial contacts, or omitted species: every admitted vertex would require one B, so no such limit avoids this B-ring obstruction.
