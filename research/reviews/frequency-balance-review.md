**Re-review status:** this record includes repairs or extensions made during
review. A separate independent review of the resulting version is not

**Artifact reviewed:** [frequency-balance.md](../lemmas/frequency-balance.md).

**Date:** 2026-10-02
**Verdict:** **Valid as a necessary real-frequency system.**

The review required centroid anchoring, per-area normalization, and explicit
boundary-strip errors.  A finite protoset bounds local counts; positive
minimum corner angle makes the catalog of labeled vertex multisets finite.
Corner and side incidences and Euler's equation survive along a convergent
square Følner subsequence.

Reflections may be tracked by a parity flag.  Symmetric prototiles can have
nonunique reference labels, but any consistent choices produce a refined
solution, and aggregate balances remain necessary.  Absolute rotations may
vary continuously and are deliberately not part of this finite certificate.

The review rejected stronger interpretations: variables need not be
integers or positive; a sparse globally present type can have zero density;
positive-density vertex types, not necessarily every exceptional vertex,
obey conclusions derived from averaged valence; and LP feasibility is not a
global tiling or periodicity certificate.  Strong edge-to-edge contact is
essential unless subdivided-side flags are introduced.
