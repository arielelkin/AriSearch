# Fixed-angle deformation transfers a periodic hexagon escape

Date: 2026-10-15. Status: independently hostile-reviewed VALID;
[review](../reviews/hexagon-fixed-angle-contact-deformation-hostile-2026-10-15.md). This is a restricted lemma, not an unrestricted impossibility theorem.

## Statement

Let A,B be distinct genuine convex hexagons, each with at least five distinct native side lengths. Suppose T is an edge-to-edge, everywhere-trivalent tiling by these shapes. Index the sides of every tile occurrence by an isometry from its native reference polygon; resolve reference symmetries consistently for each occurrence, with no claim of canonical indexing.

Let E be the equivalence relation on the twelve native side slots generated by all full-side pairings occurring in T. For every E-class C let x_C be its common original length. Let u_Ai and u_Bj be the native directed unit edge vectors, in positively oriented boundary order. Define the real linear map

    M: R^E -> R^4,
    M(v)=(sum_i v_[Ai] u_Ai, sum_j v_[Bj] u_Bj).

Suppose there is v in ker M such that the numbers v_[Ai] differ on every pair of equal-length, distinct A slots. (When A has five distinct lengths there is only one pair to separate.) Then the original unmarked pair admits a doubly periodic full-side tiling.

The hypothesis is also satisfied if the corresponding condition holds for B. If A already has six distinct lengths, the separation condition is empty and the reviewed A6/B5 theorem applies directly. Thus the only new case here is A5/B5.

## Proof

Write x(epsilon)=x+epsilon v. Since Mx=Mv=0, the successive vectors x_Ai(epsilon)u_Ai close to form a polygon A_epsilon, and similarly for B_epsilon. For sufficiently small nonzero epsilon, all lengths are positive and each resulting indexed polygon is genuine strict convex: all strict support inequalities of the original polygons remain strict by continuity. Native edge directions and interior angles are unchanged.

Choose epsilon sufficiently small that originally different lengths remain different. The assumed separation then makes all six A_epsilon lengths distinct. B_epsilon has at least five distinct lengths. Distinctness of the two congruence classes persists for small epsilon because two distinct finite polygons have positive distance modulo Euclidean isometries in a fixed compact normalization. Alternatively, an accidental equality of the two classes would itself give the monohedral periodic escape through the same transfer below.

Give the abstract faces of T the new polygon metrics and glue precisely the same indexed full sides. Every glued pair belongs to one E-class and has the same new length. The result is topologically the original plane. The metric is locally Euclidean on face interiors, on edge interiors, and at vertices: the trivalent vertex angle sums remain exactly2pi.

It is complete. Triangulate each native original and perturbed polygon by joining an interior point (for example its vertex average) to every vertex. The corresponding piecewise affine maps have finite positive upper and lower length bounds. There are only two finite triangulation templates, so one uniform bilipschitz constant works on all faces, including reflected occurrences. On a glued side, both affine boundary maps are the same linear map to the corresponding new segment; hence these maps agree and form a global bilipschitz homeomorphism from the old plane with its Euclidean length metric to the new intrinsic length metric. Completeness follows. A complete simply connected flat surface develops isometrically onto the Euclidean plane. Therefore A_epsilon,B_epsilon admit a full-side plane tiling.

Apply the reviewed theorem in hexagon-six-five-general-periodic-escape-2026-10-15.md. Its conclusion and proofs provide a doubly periodic full-side tiling, either through an explicit compact flat torus or through intrinsic deterministic full-side propagation and a rank-two translation lattice. In the latter case take its finite translation quotient. In both cases retain the finite indexed polygon side pairing of a compact flat torus.

Replace every perturbed face by its original indexed polygon and keep that pairing. Every paired numerical length equality of the perturbed polygons also held originally: epsilon was chosen to prevent any equality between originally different lengths. Thus all paired original sides have equal lengths. Every quotient vertex still has the same angle sum2pi. The quotient remains the same connected orientable topological torus, endowed with a flat metric. Its universal cover is the Euclidean plane, and its rank-two translation deck group yields a doubly periodic tiling by A,B. This transfers a single exact witness; no limit of periods or uniform face bound is needed. QED.

## Linear obstruction certificate

For A5, let i,k be its two repeated native slots. Let d be the coordinate functional v -> v_[Ai]-v_[Ak], taking d=0 if the slots are already E-equivalent. A separating deformation exists exactly when d is nonzero on ker M, equivalently d is NOT in the row space of M. Therefore failure is an exact rank condition:

    rank(M with row d appended)=rank(M).

The same holds for B. An aperiodic A5/B5 pair must satisfy both rank obstructions in every admitted defect-free tiling. These conditions are necessary only; they do not construct a tiling or establish aperiodicity. When coordinates are exact algebraic numbers, M and its ranks can be computed exactly after adjoining the edge-length square roots. The theorem itself permits arbitrary exact real geometric data and uses real linear algebra.

## Dependencies and review boundary

The unrestricted hypothesis that the pair admits some plane tiling may be reduced to T by the reviewed defect-free hexagon limit theorem. If the limit uses just one type, the monohedral hexagon periodic escape already closes that branch; the theorem above is useful for a mixed limit. Needed dependencies: complete flat-surface development; the reviewed A6/B5 periodic-escape theorem; exact preservation of numerical equalities and native vertex angles under the transferred finite pairing. No finite-model property is assumed.

A fresh reviewer should attack global completeness of the re-metricized plane, face indexing under symmetries, the full-side nature of the deterministic quotient, and the implication from a perturbed side equality to its original equality. The auxiliary formal-color strengthening in the accompanying direction note is NOT needed for this proof.
