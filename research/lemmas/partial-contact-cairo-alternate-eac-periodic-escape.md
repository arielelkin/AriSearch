# A periodic escape at the alternate e=a+c resonance

**Status:** freshly independently hostile-reviewed **VALID**, with exact
raw-coordinate/quotient development; see [review](../reviews/partial-contact-cairo-alternate-eac-periodic-review.md). This settles one fixed alternate pair by a
periodic tiling, not the main research target.

Let w=sqrt3, a=1 and

    b=1+4w/3, c=w/3, d=2+w/3,
    e=1+w/3=a+c, f=2+4w/3=a+b, h=1-w/3=a-c.

Let P=(a,b,c,d,e), Q=(a,d,b,c,f), with native side directions
0,90,150,240,300degrees and native angles after sides
90,120,90,120,120. All listed lengths are positive. These are the
strictly convex shapes already checked at this exact ratio in the complete
alternate-domain certificate. Arbitrary positive common scaling gives the
same construction.

**Claim.** This pair admits a periodic plane tiling by translations,
rotations and reflections. Native partial contacts are used.

## Explicit quotient

The [eight-face quotient certificate](../certificates/partial-contact-cairo-alternate-eac-eight-face-lift.json)
contains all8 directed face boundaries and24 paired directed side pieces.
There are four copies of P and four copies of Q, two reflected copies of
each species. Each P has its native a split into(c,h) and its native e
split into(c,a); each Q stays whole. The first unreflected P boundary is

    lengths: c,h,b,c,d,c,a
    angles before those sides:120,180,90,120,90,120,180.

The unreflected Q boundary is(a,d,b,c,f) with angles before sides
(120,90,120,90,120). For a reflected copy, reverse the side order and
reverse the corner order keeping vertex0 fixed. The certificate labels
the faces0,1 as unreflected P,2,3 as reflected P,4,5 as unreflected Q,
and6,7 as reflected Q.

Every side piece is used exactly once in the24 pairings. Paired pieces
have equal exact length, and their starts are identified with the other
piece's ends. This orientation-reversing boundary identification glues
oriented face interiors consistently. The dual face graph is connected.

There are16 quotient vertex classes, each containing three corner
intervals with total angle360. For each class, the actual link graph is
formed by its corner intervals and the endpoint gluings from the paired
side pieces. Every corner interval has two link endpoints, the gluing
matches each endpoint exactly once, and the graph is connected and
of degree2. Thus each class has one circular link. Merely summing its
angles without this circular-link check would be insufficient.

Consequently the quotient is a compact, connected, orientable Euclidean
surface without singularities or boundary. Its Euler characteristic is
16-24+8=0, so it is a flat torus. Its complete universal cover is the
Euclidean plane. The lifted face interiors are disjoint and their closures
cover it; all artificial straight vertices are precisely native partial
contacts. Each lifted face is a rigid copy of the original P or Q.

An orientation-preserving nontranslation Euclidean isometry has a fixed
point; deck transformations of this covering have none. Its deck group
therefore acts by translations and is a rank2 lattice. The resulting
plane tiling is periodic. No finite local coverage or marginal balance
is substituted for this global surface argument.

## Exact development and periods

The [developed certificate](../certificates/partial-contact-cairo-alternate-eac-eight-face-development.json)
separately encodes exact reference vertices, all8 rigid placements,
all24 side-cycle holonomies, and the integral lattice generated by those
holonomies. Values are coefficient pairs in Q(w); no numerical tolerance
is used for geometry or equality checks. It gives period generators

    U=(2+w,2+w), V=(4w/3,-4-10w/3).

All side-cycle rotational holonomies are identity. The integer coefficient
matrix of all translations has rational rank2; integer row Hermite normal
form computes the full generated subgroup, without dropping a possibly
nonprimitive cycle. The absolute determinant of U,V is

    22+40w/3.

The exact native areas are

    area(P)=3/2+5w/3, area(Q)=4+5w/3.

Hence the lattice covolume equals4area(P)+4area(Q), the total motif area.
This agrees with the degree1 torus development and precludes hidden
multiplicity in the claimed fundamental motif.
