# A boundary-point dual construction necessarily creates a triangular escape

Date: 2026-10-12. **Adversarially reviewed:** [fresh hostile review](../reviews/penrose-boundary-dual-spoke-hostile-review.md).
This is a new narrow obstruction, not a resolution of the two-convex-tile
problem and not an enlargement of the recorded Ammann enumeration.

## Exact mechanism

Let R be a locally finite edge-to-edge tiling by compact convex polygons,
each written without redundant collinear vertices. For every source tile r,
choose a point p_r strictly inside r. For every source edge e, choose a
point q_e strictly inside e, shared by its two incident tiles. The choices
may depend arbitrarily on neighbours, or even on the whole source tiling;
no congruence or locality condition is assumed.

Within r draw the segment p_r q_e for every boundary edge e of r. Erase all
source edges. The final network G consists only of these spokes, whose
vertices are p_r and q_e. Declare its faces to be the intended final tile
interiors and their closures to be the intended compact polygons.

This family is outside the earlier 98-type rhomb domain because its
segments end at source-edge interiors and its shared q_e can depend on
both incident rhombs. It includes the common construction where p_r is
the rhomb centre and q_e is the intersection of the centre-to-centre
segment with their common source edge, whenever that intersection is
strictly inside e.

## Theorem

If R has a source vertex of valence three, then either the faces of G do
not form a convex polygon tiling, or the final tile shapes include a
triangle. In the latter case any finite protoset containing all those
shapes admits a periodic monohedral tiling and therefore is not aperiodic.

In particular, a two-class construction of this form on any marked
Penrose source containing the exact three-star retained below cannot
solve the target problem, regardless of neighbour-dependent choices of
p_r and q_e.

## Proof

Within a source polygon r, the spokes from its interior point to the
strictly interior points of its edges occur in cyclic boundary order and
do not cross except at p_r. They split r into one region for each source
corner. The region belonging to a corner v has boundary consisting of
the two source-edge subsegments meeting at v and the two spokes through
the adjacent crossing points. On erasing source edges, these corner
regions join precisely with the corresponding regions of other source
tiles at the *same* vertex v. Thus a face of G corresponds to each source
vertex; no two distinct source vertices are joined into the same face.

If the incident source tiles in cyclic order are r_1,...,r_d and their
successive radial source edges are e_1,...,e_d, this face has a simple
boundary alternating

    p_(r_1), q_(e_1), p_(r_2), q_(e_2), ..., p_(r_d), q_(e_d).

It is bounded by the finite incident source star. Its interior is
nonempty: local finiteness and strict placement put every spoke a
positive distance away from v, so a sufficiently small open disk about
v lies entirely in this face.

Each q_e is a degree-two network vertex. Exactly two distinct faces
meet there, one for each endpoint of the source edge. Their local
angular sectors exhaust 2pi. If both face closures are convex, each
sector has angle at most pi, and hence both angles equal pi. Therefore
p_r, q_e and p_(r') lie on one straight segment, with q_e between the two
interior points. The crossing point is a boundary subdivision, not an
intrinsic polygon corner.

Delete all q_e from each boundary word. The face at a valence-d source
vertex consequently has at most d intrinsic corners, all at the p_r.
For d=3 it has at most three corners; its established positive area
forces exactly three. It is a triangle.

Every triangle tiles periodically: the triangle and its half-turn about
the midpoint of any one edge form a parallelogram; translates of that
parallelogram by its two side vectors tile the plane. This gives a
monohedral triangle tiling under the allowed Euclidean isometries.
The protoset's other shapes need not be used, so the periodic tiling is
an admitted escape. This proves the theorem. QED.

## Exact marked-Penrose fixture

`research/reviews/penrose-boundary-dual-trivalent-star.json` retains the
three rhombs numbered 0, 2 and 7 in the exact three-times-deflated
Robinson sun generated by `ammann-two-class-patchdata.py 3`.
Coordinates lie in Q(w), w=exp(i*pi/5), using the four-vector basis
1,w,w^2,w^3. They meet at the recorded vertex numbered 3 in that source
patch. Their incident angles are 144,72,144 degrees. The corresponding
corner indices are 3,2,3 in the marked rhomb frames. Thus they surround
the vertex once with no angular gap.

The standalone checker
`research/reviews/verify_penrose_boundary_dual_trivalent_star.py` uses
the recorded coordinates without importing the source producer. It
checks each rhomb's unit sides, parallelogram closure, positive turns,
pairwise radial shared edges, and distinct tile centres. For the centred
choice it solves the three centre-line/source-edge intersections exactly,
checks they lie strictly inside both segments, and checks that the centre
triangle contains the source vertex strictly. The resulting final face
is therefore concretely a genuine triangular tile, rather than merely
a possible corner-count bound.

The finite fixture certifies the local source occurrence generated by
the stated substitution. The theorem assumes an actual source tiling
containing a trivalent vertex. This record does not independently prove
the general repetitivity/minimality theorem needed to transfer one
finite legal patch to every marked Penrose tiling; it does not assert a
new theorem about arbitrary bare-rhomb tilings.

## Relation to earlier obstructions

This is not the earlier trivalent-source fusion obstruction. The source
may have many higher-valence vertices, and the operation is a graph
dualization with shared boundary points, not a union of whole source
tiles. Only a *single* trivalent source star is needed. It is also not
the unresolved one-face-per-rhomb density argument: no class frequencies,
rational density decomposition, or global counting identity occurs.

To evade this precise obstruction, a construction must change an actual
used assumption: for example its final network cannot be just the full
one-interior-point spoke dualization at that three-star. Extra network
edges/nodes, retention of source vertices, or operations that fuse the
dual faces are not covered. No two-class candidate of those changed
forms is claimed here.
