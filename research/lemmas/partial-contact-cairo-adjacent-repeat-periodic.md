## Exact shape, family member and congruent partner

gives

    P=(1,1,c,d,2), c=(3w-1)/2, d=(w-1)/2.

Its positive native vertices in the standard directions are

    P0=(0,0),P1=(1,0),P2=(1,1),
    P3=(1-wc/2,1+c/2),P4=(-1,w).

This is the r=1 metric in the exceptional family. The partner is

    Q=(d,c,1,1,2).

It is congruent to P by reflection and native reversal. Explicitly, with

    F30=((1/2,w/2),(w/2,-1/2)),

its native vertices are F30*(P_i-P4) for i=4,3,2,1,0 in that order. Thus
there is only one unmarked congruence class at this parameter. A tiling
using P alone is admitted by the original pair conventions in any case.
Scaling the witness below supplies a periodic monohedral tiling for every
Cairo shape with a=b and e=2a, since the normalized closure is unique.

## Eight-pentagon motif and periods

Define one convex quadrant pentagon T with vertices

    (0,0),(d,0),(d+1,w),(d+1/2,3w/2),(0,c).

If R120 is rotation through120 degrees, then T's listed vertices are
R120*(P_i-P3) for i=3,4,0,1,2. Thus T is exactly an isometric P copy.
Its four coordinate-axis reflections tile the nonconvex cluster S(1),
with disjoint quadrant interiors. Reflection copies are admitted.

Let Rminus90(x,y)=(y,-x). Use the eight original pentagon faces consisting
of these four reflected T copies, together with the four faces obtained
by applying Rminus90 and translating by(0,3w). The two cluster centers are
therefore0 and(0,3w), and the second cluster is quarter-turned.

Repeat this eight-face motif by the lattice generated by

    u=(w,-w), v=(3w,3w).

area9/4, so the total motif area is18, exactly the lattice covolume.

## Complete overlap and coverage proof

The retained exact checker treats the eight original convex pentagons,
not just the nonconvex outline. Write U for the matrix with columns u,v.
For every motif face, it computes the two min/max coordinate intervals of
its vertices after the invertible map U^-1. A translate of face j by
m*u+n*v can meet face i only if both transformed coordinate intervals
intersect. This gives the complete finite integer ranges

    ceil(min_i[k]-max_j[k]) <= index[k]
        <= floor(max_i[k]-min_j[k]), k=0,1.

Every potentially meeting ordered face/translation pair other than the
identical occurrence is therefore enumerated. There are exactly84 such
comparisons, with maximum absolute lattice index2. For every pair the
checker finds a separating axis among the native edge normals of either
convex pentagon, with weakly separated projection intervals. This excludes
all interior overlap, including within the motif, self translations and
all other lattice translates. Equality allows valid shared boundaries.
There is no unexplored farther translation: it would violate one of the
rigorously derived transformed bounding intervals.

The periodic interior multiplicity is at most1 almost everywhere, and its
mean is total motif area/covolume=1. It is therefore1 almost everywhere.
The complete union of the lattice translates of these closed fixed faces
is locally finite and closed. Any uncovered point would have an open
uncovered neighborhood of positive area, contradicting that multiplicity.
Thus the eight-face lattice construction covers the whole plane with
pairwise disjoint interiors. It is an admitted doubly periodic monotiling
by P.

This argument establishes a full infinite tiling. It does not extrapolate
a finite patch or assume every corner numerically closes.
