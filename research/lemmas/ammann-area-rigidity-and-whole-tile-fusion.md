## Source definitions and angle-forcing boundary

Let w be the root of w^4-w^3+w^2-w+1=0 with Re(w)>4/5 and Im(w)>0, so
w=exp(i*pi/5). Set phi=w+conjugate(w)=(1+sqrt5)/2 and sigma=Im(w)>0.
For real s,t set J=s+t*w and define

    J'=1+w^4*(J-1),       J''=w+w^6*(J-w),
    K=w^2+w^6*(J-1),      L=1+w^7*(J-1-w),
    J2=1+w+w^2-J.

The reviewed indexed source polygons are

    H6=(0,J',1,J,w,J''),
    P1=(0,J',1,L,K),
    P2=(K,L,1+w^2,J2,w^2).

The geometric domain D requires0<s,t<1 and all three polygons strictly
convex and positively oriented. Applications of source forcing require
J in the REVIEWED generic domain G* defined in
[ammann-forcing-aperiodicity.md](ammann-forcing-aperiodicity.md): four distinct
edge labels(G1) and absence of nonidentity nonnegative180/360 angle sums(G2').
The sufficient condition(G2) is Q-linear independence of1,chi/pi,psi/pi,kappa/pi.
Area independence alone does NOT imply membership in G*, convexity or source
aperiodicity. These obligations are separately certified below at one point.

## Universal areas and exact rank criterion

For every s,t the oriented shoelace areas, normalized by sigma, are

    area(H6)/sigma=1,
    area(P1)/sigma=phi-f(s,t),
    area(P2)/sigma=f(s,t),
    f(s,t)=(s^2+phi*s*t+t^2+(phi-2)*t)/2.

Proof: substitute the displayed affine vertex lists into
area(P)/sigma=sum(conjugate(p_i)*p_(i+1)-p_i*conjugate(p_(i+1)))/(2(w-conjugate(w))).
Conjugation fixes s,t and sends w to w^-1. Reducing powers by Phi10 gives the
three displayed polynomials identically. The independent verifier reconstructs
that exact calculation with no source-producer import. In D these are the
positive UNSIGNED areas relevant to congruence, including reflected copies.

There is no universal homogeneous rational relation between the three areas.
Indeed the rational coefficients at(constant1,constantphi,s^2) of these three
polynomials form rows

    (1,0,0), (0,1,-1/2), (0,0,1/2),

whose determinant is1/2. Note the relation area(P1)+area(P2)=phi*area(H6)
has an IRRATIONAL coefficient; it is not an integer-content ambiguity.

At ANY fixed parameter, the three areas have rational rank3 exactly when
f(s,t) is not in Q(phi). Proof: a rational relation with coefficients(qH,q1,q2)
reduces after dividing by sigma to

    qH+q1*phi+(q2-q1)*f=0.

If f is outside Q(phi), then q2=q1, after which irrationality ofphi forces
qH=q1=q2=0. Conversely, if f=A+B*phi for rationals A,B, the rank is exactly2
because1 and phi are in the span and all three areas are in Q(phi). The
rational relation space is generated by

    (-A,-B,1-B).

Clearing denominators gives its primitive integer content-difference vector.
Thus the criterion classifies the complete pointwise rational area-rank gate.
For rational s,t the rank is necessarily2; existing rational samples cannot
establish rank3. At the reviewed base point(s,t)=(9/20,3/4),
f=-147/400+(87/160)phi and the primitive vector is(294,-435,365).
Equal-area different-content clusters there must differ by an integer multiple
of that vector. Equal area is necessary, not sufficient for congruence.

The rank3 criterion holds outside countably many f-level curves. In0<s,t<1,
partial f/partial s=s+(phi/2)t>0, so each level has locally dimension1.
Intersecting the complement with open G* gives a dense full-measure set of
area-rigid parameters, but it need not be open. This distinguishes rational
area independence from the already reviewed open generic angle condition.

## Congruent whole-source clusters have fixed content

Fix ANY J in G* with f not in Q(phi). Let two finite patches of whole source
tiles, with pairwise disjoint interiors, have congruent supports. Write their
integer source content vectors u,v. Congruence preserves unsigned area even
under reflection, so

    (uH-vH)area(H6)+(u1-v1)area(P1)+(u2-v2)area(P2)=0.

Area independence forces u=v. Multiple geometric dissections of the same
macro support may remain, but none has a different content vector. No
recognizability or unique spatial dissection hypothesis is needed.

Therefore EVERY final congruence class in a whole-source-tile compiler has
one fixed content vector. Uniform cluster diameter follows from a fixed compact
macro support; positive minimum source area bounds its tile count. This does
not require imposing a content restriction as an extra matching rule.

In particular, a macro class congruent to P1 has content exactly(0,1,0),
and one congruent to P2 has content exactly(0,0,1), since those singleton
patches already realize their areas. Thus for this area-rigid source, allowing
a same-shaped alternative whole-tile dissection cannot evade singleton status.

## Singleton-pentagon two-class fusion is impossible, even with proposed variable content

Suppose an actual admitted source tiling at such J partitions tile by tile
into two final congruence classes, one congruent to P1 or P2, the other a
compact macro support composed of whole source tiles. All tiles must be used
once; no cuts, overlaps, fractional transfers or added pieces are permitted.
The preceding area argument makes both contents fixed automatically.
[The freshly reviewed anchored count theorem](ammann-anchored-count-split-fixed-cluster-obstruction.md)
gives p1/h,p2/h ->phi for every admitted source tiling. If P1 remains singleton,
H and P2 can appear only in the other class, so their ratio is a rational
integer-content ratio, contradictingphi. The P2 case is identical.
Hence no such partition exists, regardless of macro side count, convexity,
reflections, or whether it was advertised as having variable source content.
This conclusion holds for the explicit N=1000 source and for every G* source
satisfying the parameter-uniform area criterion.

For two nontrivial macro classes the earlier necessary conditions remain:
their now automatically fixed content vectors must be independent, balanced
in P1/P2 counts individually, and have a nonnegative cone containing the
limiting source direction(1,phi,phi). This does not exclude those balanced
compilers or produce their geometry. A new route must change the singleton
shape assumption, allow source cuts/fractions, or change the source mechanism.
Rank2 kernel-compatible contents pass only this LOCAL area gate; they do not
by themselves justify reopening a two-macro compiler. The companion
[uniform-resection proof](ammann-supported-pair-uniform-resection-obstruction.md)
shows that the unmarked source conventions strengthen the singleton exclusion
to every G* parameter without area independence, and force all dissections of
any tileable two-macro pair to be balanced and fixed-content. That analytic argument has a separate fresh independent VALID review.
