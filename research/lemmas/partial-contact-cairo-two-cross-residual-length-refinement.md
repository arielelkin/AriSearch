# Residual-piece lengths in the two-cross Cairo native seam model

**Status: exact completed finite test, fresh independent review pending.**
This model is necessary only on the explicitly stated additional generic
stratum; it has produced no new obstruction in the first tested families.

The reviewed native contact theorem gives at most one T in a native side.
A subdivided arm has one piece carrying its native120 endpoint and one
residual piece between native90 and T180. The latter has zero120 endpoints.
After all native sides are subdivided at actual T points, no new original
vertex can lie strictly inside one of these pieces: it would be another
native-side T, or a120-in-side impossible half-star. Hence each actual
contact pairs entire original subdivision pieces with equal lengths.

The earlier safe optional model deliberately ignored the lengths of
zero120 pieces. The new refinement attaches the full formal length vector
to those pieces as well. Actual arm-order choices still ensure every
residual is positive. For a fixed open original event flat and strict
arm-order chamber, reconstruct all optional native variants. A residual
from native arm_i cut by arm_j has form arm_i-arm_j. Collect every distinct
such form, and every pairwise difference. Retain the nonidentity difference
planes in both full kernel and original eight-arm coordinates. On the
additional stratum avoiding all these proper planes, two pieces have equal
actual length exactly when their full formal forms are identical. Thus
the full-length color model is a valid necessary relation on that stratum.
The original open flat may contain a residual-equality exceptional plane,
so a generic dual could not exclude the whole flat without deeper coverage.

The producer tests all158 strict order chambers of the four remaining
dimension3 families and all16 retained normalized ratio chambers of the
separated46/49 families. Every chamber has exactly one proper residual-
difference plane. Positive fixtures are explicitly perturbed off all proper
original event and residual planes while preserving their strict order and,
for separated cases, the original ratio interval.

All174 full-color models have exact necessary primals; there are no duals.
Therefore this refinement supplies no new tileability exclusion in the
complete stated test domain. A necessary primal is not a tiling. For five
of the six families that sole plane is already an undeclared arm/privacy
plane and often an old event plane. Only orbit7flat12 introduces a genuine
new residual plane, proportional to Pb+Pd-Qa-Qd=0 in native coordinates.
Its exceptional metrics remain outside this generic test. No full additional
intersection-lattice search or whole-family conclusion is asserted.
