# Whole outer sides exclude integer multiples of phi as expansion

**Status:** adversarially reviewed as valid, including the conditional scale spectrum, in the [fresh audit](../reviews/pentagrid-short-boundary-obstruction-review.md). This is a finite dissection obstruction for one exact metric pair, not an impossibility theorem for plane tilings.

## Shapes and statement

Use the b=c=1 member of [the area-compatible family](../searches/pentagrid-golden-metric-area-compatible.md): U has side lengths (phi^3,phi^3,1,1,1) and angles after the sides (1,3,4,4,3), while V has side lengths (1,3phi+1,1,2phi,2phi) and angles after the sides (2,2,4,3,4), in units pi/5. Exact directions and vertices are given in that artifact and [its certificate](../certificates/pentagrid-golden-metric-area-compatible.json). Reflections and arbitrary partial contacts are permitted.

**Theorem.** For any positive integer m, the homothetic parent (m*phi)U cannot be partitioned into finitely many isometric copies of U and V. In particular the area-compatible 2phi inflation with matrix [[4,4],[4,8]], requiring eight children in the U parent, has no geometric realization.

## Whole child edges on the outer boundary

In a finite convex-polygon partition of a convex parent, each genuine parent side is a concatenation of whole child sides. To see this, any positive-length child contact on a parent supporting line lies on a child side. That whole side remains on the same line. It cannot continue past a genuine parent endpoint: the line beyond that endpoint lies outside the parent, whereas the child is contained in the parent. Distinct children cannot overlap in the relative interiors of such boundary edges, because both interiors lie on the same side of the parent supporting line and their local half-disks would overlap. Their finitely many whole sides therefore concatenate to exactly the parent side. Internal partial contacts do not change this outer-boundary fact.

## Exact golden length sum

The child side alphabet is

    1, 2phi, 1+2phi, 1+3phi.

Each coefficient in the basis (1,phi) is nonnegative. A short side of the parent (m*phi)U has length m*phi, whose rational coefficient is0. Rational independence of 1 and phi forces every child edge along that side to have rational coefficient0. Thus every such edge is a V edge of length2phi. There must be exactly m/2 of them. Odd m is immediately impossible.

For even m, consider either144-degree parent corner between two consecutive short sides. The child edge ending at that corner on each parent side is a V edge of length2phi. The endpoints of those V edges have angles108 or144 degrees, never72: V sides3 and4 have endpoint pairs (144,108) and (108,144).

If the two terminal edges belong to different children, the two child corner sectors inside the parent already have total angle at least216 degrees, exceeding the parent144-degree aperture. If they belong to the same child, they must be its two adjacent2phi edges. Those meet at a108-degree corner; the two parent boundary rays meet at144 degrees. One convex child cannot have the same two boundary rays with a different interior aperture. Either alternative is impossible. This proves the theorem for every even m, completing the proof.

The argument uses actual corner/edge geometry and an exact two-dimensional length sum. It does not infer aperiodicity from an irrational length and does not use any bounded patch search.

## Exact conditional expansion spectrum

For this fixed b=c=1 fixture, any finite homothetic dissection with scale lambda>1 has a parent side of lengthlambda. The whole-boundary argument writes lambda as a nonnegative integer combination of child lengths. Therefore directly

    lambda=p+q*phi, p,q nonnegative integers,
    (p,q) belongs to the additive semigroup generated by
    (1,0),(0,2),(1,2),(1,3).

No algebraic-integer theorem is needed for this particular fixture. The phi coefficient q cannot equal1. If p=0, the theorem above excludes every possible q. If q=0, lambda is an integer and irrationality of area(V)/area(U)=phi forces every child in a U-parent dissection to be U. Such a nontrivial finite reptile dissection produces arbitrarily large complete U patches by iteration and hence a monohedral U plane tiling by compactness. This contradicts the explicitly imported complete pentagon classification in [the reviewed monohedral filter](pentagrid-pentagon-monohedral-filter.md); this last exclusion is conditional on that named classification, rather than an elementary length consequence.

Consequently, under that imported classification, any possible nontrivial homothetic substitution of this fixture must satisfy

    p>=1, q>=2, lambda>=1+2phi=phi^3.

This is a necessary scale spectrum, not existence at the lower bound. It excludes phi and phi^2 by the semigroup q=1 gate, and excludes2phi by the terminal-corner theorem. The more general algebraic-field hypotheses are kept separately in [the generic metric substitution obstruction](pentagrid-generic-metric-substitution-obstruction.md).

## Scope

The excluded 2phi proposal has area-compatible counts U parent4U+4V and V parent4U+8V, since area(V)=phi*area(U). The obstruction already occurs on the U parent boundary, so no second-parent search is needed. This exact candidate was rejected before launching a placement solver.

Expansions with a nonzero rational coefficient, including phi^3=1+2phi, permit other boundary words and are not excluded here. The theorem says nothing about nonhierarchical plane tilings by U,V, compound supertiles, or another metric family. Those remain separate questions.
