## Congruence completeness

Delete assigned flat vertices. For each pair of equal-size reduced polygons, consider every cyclic shift and both cyclic directions. A candidate bijection is congruent exactly when every pairwise Euclidean squared distance is equal, using d(P,Q)=(xP-xQ)^2+3(yP-yQ)^2. This includes orientation-reversing isometries. Convex polygon isometries must preserve cyclic adjacency up to reversal, so the finite list is exhaustive.

Require a partition of the four sectors into two nonempty sets with every pair within a set congruent. There are seven such bipartitions, all tested in one disjunction. This expresses at most two classes, including the one-class case. Thus UNSAT excludes exactly-two-class dissections as well. No extra edge matching, chirality, or orientation assumption is used.
