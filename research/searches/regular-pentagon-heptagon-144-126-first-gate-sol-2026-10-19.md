## Shape-only local constraints

The nonnegative angle equation 108p+144a+126b=360 has exactly (p,a,b)=(2,1,0),(1,0,2). There is no solution to 108p+144a+126b=180 with any corner. Thus a corner cannot meet an edge interior; unrestricted plane tilings would be edge-to-edge and trivalent. At A the vertex is PPQ; at B it is PQQ. Every B has one incident QQ side. On the path of six B vertices the unique perfect matching is e1,e3,e5. The four remaining Q sides face P and hence have length one. PP sides also have length one. This derives the metric family without imposed decorations or contact rules.

There is only one cyclic angle word ABBBBBB. Reflections remain permitted throughout. Monohedral tilings by either tile alone are excluded by the angle equation: neither has an all-one-type vertex. This does not exclude a mixed periodic tiling. Vertex counting in a periodic quotient would require equal numbers of P and Q; it gives no contradiction. No simple periodic witness is claimed or excluded. No torus search was run.
