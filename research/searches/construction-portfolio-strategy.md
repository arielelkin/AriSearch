# Construction portfolio after the hexagon family closures

Date: 2026-10-13. Status: research strategy, not a candidate or a theorem.
Requested alongside frontier consolidation and the two residual B5+1 proofs.
The unrestricted problem remains [research-problem.md](../../research-problem.md).

## Decision and allocation

Make an exact infinite covering the first constructive milestone. Reserve at
least half of the next research cycle for construction, with one independent
falsification/review role. Complete the concrete B5+1 closure obligations, then
pause that family rather than replacing it with adjacent parameter sweeps.
A proposed allocation after this checkpoint is 50% construction, 25% targeted
structural work, and 25% verification/hostile review; after B5+1 closes, move
its share to construction. These are priorities, not a scheduled automation. The first cycle admits one
explicit surgery for Track A and one explicit parent scheme for Track B,
with at most one specifically justified repair to each. Fix each finite
local domain before computation; failure after that repair triggers a
portfolio decision, not automatic domain growth.

Maintain two genuinely different construction mechanisms below. Each starts
with a written finite mathematical question and a named obstruction it changes.
Do not allocate compute to a mechanism whose only proposal is "find a hierarchy"
or "increase the patch". Gate failure ends that particular proposal, not the
unrestricted problem.

## Shared candidate contract

Every proposed pair must have a short ledger with these separate obligations:

1. Exact positive-area convex polygons, exactly two congruence classes including
   reflected congruence; remove collinear corners before counting sides.
2. Existence: either an exact globally compatible transformation of a known
   infinite source tiling, or an explicit extension/compactness theorem for the
   proposed hierarchy. A list of unrelated finite patches is insufficient.
3. An intrinsic decoder or parent rule, derived from the unmarked polygons and
   valid in every admitted tiling. Enumerate or analytically control reflections,
   partial contacts, alternative assemblies, and omission of either species.
4. A proof excluding every nonzero translation, including one-direction periods.
   A nonperiodic selected tiling or absence of a small torus is insufficient.
5. Exact finite certificates with independent checks, plus a primary-literature
   boundary before any novelty claim.

Test positive monohedral criteria first: one admitted periodic single-shape
tiling kills the pair immediately. Failing known sufficient criteria does not
by itself prove the absence of monohedral tilings; the final universal proof
must also exclude omission. Use the corner-resource and shortest-side results
as cheap falsifiers before solving larger metric systems.

## Track A: change incidence in an existing infinite source resection

### Starting object and actual change

Use the exact infinite Ammann H/D precursor in
[the incidence obstruction](../lemmas/ammann-hd-incidence-preserving-convexification-obstruction.md).
It supplies existence before convexification, although D is nonconvex.
Ordinary notch filling and vertex movement are already excluded.

The new proposal must put a THIRD final face at the forced two-face H vertices,
or split/reassign a face across source boundaries. Permit new branching points
and genuine source-edge crossings by final faces. Both changes must appear in
an explicit local boundary diagram with exact symbolic coordinates; merely
renaming the old cap transfer does not qualify.

### First work package

Write one concrete local surgery at a required H-J'/H-J'' configuration,
including its neighbouring faces. Specify every final boundary segment, final
face identity, variable domain, and how the surgery agrees with its translated
or reflected neighbour. Use exact affine positions on specified source edges
or exact coordinates, rather than free numerical point clouds.

Before solving congruence, answer:

- Does every resulting final face have at least five genuine corners?
- Does the proposal actually change the offending degree-two incidence?
- Can neighbouring surgeries share a boundary without crossing, gap or overlap?
- Are there at most two proposed complete boundary words under all required
  source environments, not just in the first pictured star?

Reject a failed topology before metric enumeration. If it passes, solve closure,
positive lengths, strict convexity, and congruence for those boundary words.
Retain the exact parameter domain and every exceptional equality.

### Infinite covering gate

Give a uniform bounded-radius rule on an already proved infinite source
tiling. Prove its local compatibility on a COMPLETE source neighbourhood
atlas sufficient for that rule; the fourteen vertex stars alone need not
determine larger neighbourhoods. Prove final faces are bounded, every point is
covered exactly once away from shared boundaries, and exactly two full shape
classes occur. Exhausting one finite source patch does not establish this.

The next artifact must therefore be an exact two-shape infinite resection
certificate/theorem, or a specific obstruction to the proposed surgery.
Do not restart generic hat/Penrose decoration enumeration.

### Why existing obstructions do not automatically decide this proposal

| Existing obstruction | Hypothesis the new proposal must demonstrably change |
| --- | --- |
| Source-by-source two-area module | A final tile crosses a source boundary; the sources are not separately dissected into whole final tiles |
| Balanced whole-source macro parity | Final faces contain fractional source pieces, rather than unions of whole source tiles |
| H/D incidence-preserving convexification | A required third final face/new branching incidence is introduced |
| Fixed-point Penrose exterior-angle budget | If that source is used instead, delete/change the prescribed source points or introduce actual cuts; moving within its excluded interval is insufficient |

Changing these hypotheses removes these particular proofs of failure; it is
not evidence that a construction exists. Record any new conservation relation
created by the surgery and test it before enlarging the domain.

### Universal forcing gate

In parallel with the surgery, specify a local intrinsic way to recover source
edges or source macrotiles from arbitrary final tilings. The decoded tiling must lie in the exact source class whose aperiodicity is
proved, with its required genericity or matching hypotheses; agreement with
a finite source atlas alone does not prove this. Prove uniqueness, or a
canonical translation-equivariant choice, if several decodings are possible.
If two source labels
collapse to the same unmarked boundary profile, resolve that ambiguity or
exhibit a periodic escape. Prove the decoder is defined everywhere, compatible
across contacts, and translation-equivariant. Then every period of a final
tiling would be a period of its decoded aperiodic source.

If exact existence succeeds but no concrete decoder survives the first
reflected/partial-contact attack, retain it as a tileable pair and stop this
construction claim. Do not keep refining a preferred-source renderer.

## Track B: intrinsic hierarchy with pentagon-containing tiles

### Different mechanism

Design parent recognition directly from the two shapes' contacts, without
importing Ammann or Penrose source boundaries. Start with two pentagons, or
one pentagon and a higher-sided partner justified by its role. Allow a finite
set of nonconvex parent patches made from those SAME two convex shapes;
intermediate parent types do not count as extra final prototiles, but their
labels must be geometrically recognizable, never imposed matching rules.

This is a research direction, not an already supplied geometric seed.
The first assignment is to supply one explicit parent-boundary/contact
scheme and its recognition argument. Without that scheme it is blocked,
and does not justify a general polygon search.

### First work package and gates

1. Specify two or more exact parent patches as boundary words in unknown
   native edge vectors, together with their placements and proposed next-level
   assembly. Write the closure and shared-boundary equations before solving.
2. Derive native corner sums, partial-contact possibilities, and required
   length equalities. Apply hosted-corner capacity, whole-side continuation,
   and periodic-omission attacks to this exact scheme. A positive corner
   ledger is only necessary.
3. Find exact convex atomic polygons realizing the scheme. Check every seam,
   positive area, overlap and gap analytically or with exact certificates.
4. Prove a repeatable extension: a legal seed has nested extensions containing
   discs of radii tending to infinity, with compatible placements. Alternatively
   give a properly stated compactness argument with uniformly controlled tiles.
5. Prove every admitted tiling has the intrinsic parent decomposition at every
   level. Handle fault lines, one-sided limits, reflection choices and omitted
   species explicitly.

For an ordinary similarity substitution whose parents are the two pentagons
themselves, apply the reviewed
[positive-conjugate bound](../lemmas/two-pentagon-positive-conjugate-substitution-obstruction.md):
a quadratic irrational area factor with positive conjugate must be at least
six. Reusing linear inflation phi or 1+sqrt(2) is excluded in that scope.
Nonconvex parent patches change the whole-pentagon-parent hypothesis; they
still require their own exact area/count and boundary compatibility checks.

### The nonperiodicity obligation

A hierarchy alone is not enough. One concrete sufficient target is an
intrinsically recognizable, nonempty set of level-n markers in EVERY tiling,
translation-equivariant and separated by d_n with d_n tending to infinity.
A nonzero period t would carry a marker to a distinct marker at distance
|t| for every n, contradicting d_n>|t|. The actual scheme must prove the
marker properties; they cannot be assumed from a substitution picture.
This rules out even a single translational period.

One-dimensional aperiodic row sequences alone do not meet this gate:
they may leave a translation along the rows. Any row-based proposal must
explain how the geometry also removes that remaining direction.

## Source acquisition is a bounded supporting task

The six/nine convex-pentagon recompositions identified in
[the source-boundary record](../literature/2026-10-13-danzer-six-nine-convex-source-boundary.md)
are useful alternatives only after their exact definitions and unrestricted
reconstruction hypotheses are recovered. Do not spend a geometry campaign
on a figure-only source. Once recovered, compute exact area/count modules and
their rational relations before proposing a two-type compiler.

The primary [Dzuklevski paper, Section 3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2507.20867v1)
was reopened on 2026-10-13 for this strategy. Its convex forcing construction
is a technique reference; its sigma-morphic target admits periodic tilings.
It is not an aperiodic two-tile seed. No new literature completeness or
priority claim is made here.
