# Direct radial four-child inventory: deduplicated topology stop

Date: 2026-10-20. Status: **impossible by an existing reviewed theorem**. This is a restricted substitution-inventory obstruction, not a result about arbitrary tilings or pairs.

The direct radial scheme sets a convex pentagon P and convex hexagon H, with the first parent tH=3P. The proposed second parent tP=3P+H has **four** convex children, all with at least five sides.

The independently reviewed [universal convex-pentagon dissection theorem](../lemmas/six-child-topology-universal-wheel.md) says every nontrivial convex pentagon partition into children with at least five sides has at least six children. Its hypotheses apply directly to the tP image: tP is a genuine convex pentagon, the children are whole convex polygons, and partial side contacts are allowed. Thus the proposed 3P+H image is impossible for every metric, before any side equation or packing test. The companion [substitution consequences](../lemmas/six-child-topology-substitution-consequences.md) additionally show that an image with exactly six children would have all-pentagon children, so a P-parent image containing H needs at least **seven** children.

An exploratory exact scalar screen was run before this earlier theorem was surfaced; its [script](triarm-fourchild-gate-2026-10-20.sage) is retained only as redundant diagnostic work. It found no convex metric in the particular four-child radial equations. The theorem is the decisive reason to stop this inventory; no further scalar or geometric search is warranted here.
