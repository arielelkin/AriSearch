Date: 2026-10-20. This is a routing audit, not a new tiling result. The unrestricted target and the top-of-log shape-first priority remain [unresolved](../../discovery-log.md). Compare each proposed pair by its exact cyclic angle word, numerical side-length equality pattern, metric relations, and intended forcing mechanism, modulo species swap, cyclic relabeling, reflection, rotation, and common scaling. A shared label name alone is not identity; equality of exact polygon geometry is the decisive duplicate check.

## Distinct experiment lanes

1. **P5/P5: new native partial-contact geometry.** Choose exact pentagon angle words outside the two listed long-edge words, Cairo 90/120 four-arm domains, the three-angle R/B/G family, and the pentagrid U/V/W metrics. Before growing patches, prove both polygons strictly convex and neither has a consecutive supplementary-angle monohedral half-turn escape (see [two-angle reduction](../lemmas/two-angle-pentagon-pair-cairo-reduction.md)). Record a *specific* nontrivial host-side split whose endpoint continuations survive all native corner/length checks. If the same feature is merely the old acute A/A/B T-contact, change the words and document why the global budget obstruction no longer applies.

2. **Noncentral equiangular H6/H6: changed numerical corner graph.** Use at least three lengths per tile and no central member; compare the complete cyclic length words against both recorded three-length and four/four dimer seeds, and against the fixed five/four theorem. Closure requires `l0-l3=l2-l5=l4-l1` for each word; require a legal triangle in the combined unordered corner graph, including equal-length loops. The promising observation would be a newly forced *second* grouping or a branching boundary rule that survives both half-turn and mirror seam identifications. A first private-edge dimer alone repeats the stopped seed's mechanism.

3. **Mixed-side-count shape-first pair, preferably P5 with a nonregular Q6 or Q7.** Use angle words and side incidences distinct from the fixed P5/Q7 obtuse, regular-P5 144/126, and P5/Q7 power-cell families above. A pentagon–nonregular hexagon may give a different curvature/contact budget from the heavily searched obtuse P5/Q7 palettes, provided neither species has a monohedral carrier. Start from an exact unusual small patch with a named forced continuation, then check the corresponding flat torus and species omission immediately. Do not infer plane existence from patch size.

For every candidate, save the coordinate field, cyclic angle and length words, similarity/congruence normalization, convexity certificate, a small exact patch with seam maps, its competing boundary completions, and a cheap periodic-carrier attack. A candidate earns deeper search only if it has an explicit extension idea and an intrinsic feature that might constrain *all* tilings, including reflections and partial contacts. This implements the [top-of-log instruction](../../discovery-log.md) without reopening stopped bounded negative searches.
