# Broad obtuse two-polygon periodicity: corrected gate and dual sine holonomy

Date: 2026-10-19. Status: **bounded analytic cycle; blocked**. Internal derivations only, not independently reviewed. No theorem of general periodicity, counterexample to general periodicity, literature novelty claim, enumeration, or solver experiment results.

## Scope and correction

The original proposed hypothesis placed all native angles in `(120°,180°)`. This is vacuously untileable: the reviewed [obtuse forcing theorem](../lemmas/obtuse-edge-forcing.md) forces precisely three genuine sectors at every vertex, whose sum would exceed 360°. The corrected first gate `(90°,120°]` is nonvacuous, but particularly rigid. At each vertex three angles bounded above by 120° sum to 360°, hence each equals 120°. Every corner of an occurring tile occurs in some vertex, so every occurring prototile is equiangular with angle120° and has exactly six sides. A prototile having any smaller angle cannot occur at all. This conclusion includes tilings omitting one type.

This observation is not a proof that an arbitrary pair of equiangular hexagons has a periodic escape. Their distinct positive side lengths remain unconstrained by that observation. In particular, one cannot invoke a monohedral hexagon theorem until a monohedral tiling has actually been established.

For the broader interval `(90°,180°)`, unrestricted contacts force full native-edge matches and trivalent stars by the cited theorem. Angle sum forces each polygon to have at least five sides. Existing square-window side curvature reduces possible aperiodic pairs to `(6,6)` and `(5,n)` with `n≥7`, after the already recorded sparse-type/monohedral escapes. Those are reviewed existing reductions, not contributions of this cycle. ## A different dual metric diagnostic

Let an actual full-edge trivalent tiling be given. Its cellular dual is a plane triangulation: each dual vertex corresponds to a tile and each dual triangle to a primal trivalent vertex. At the dual corner corresponding to a primal tile corner of angle `θ`, assign angle `β=π−θ`.

These are positive angles. Every dual triangle has angle sum `3π−2π=π`. At a dual vertex corresponding to an n-gon, the assigned angles sum `nπ−(n−2)π=2π`. Thus complementary angles give an abstract Euclidean triangle angle structure with exactly zero angular curvature at every dual vertex, even in the pentagon/higher-polygon case. This metric angle structure is different from the equilateral dual metric, which has combinatorial curvature `(6−n)π/3` at that vertex. Cancelling combinatorial curvature does not determine a geometric development.

There is an additional exact obstruction to realizing those assigned angles by mutually consistent positive dual edge lengths. Enumerate the primal corners `x_i` and sides `e_i=[x_i,x_{i+1}]` of a tile T counterclockwise, indices modulo n, and let U_i be the neighbor across e_i. Write `φ_i^-` and `φ_i^+` for the native angles of U_i at x_i and x_{i+1}. A necessary and sufficient length-compatibility condition is, at every tile T,

`H(T) = ∏_i sin(φ_i^+) / sin(φ_i^-) = 1`.

Necessity follows from the sine rule. In the dual triangle at x_i, let r_i be the length of dual edge T--U_i. Then

`r_i/r_{i−1} = sin(φ_{i−1}^+)/sin(φ_i^−)`.

Multiplying around T gives H(T)=1. The sine of the complementary angle equals the sine of the original angle, so no sign convention is hidden here. All factors are strictly positive under the stated angle assumptions.

For sufficiency, choose a scale for one dual triangle and propagate triangle scale across adjacent dual triangles using equality of their common edge lengths. A closed propagation circuit around a dual vertex has multiplicative discrepancy H(T) or its reciprocal, hence is consistent exactly when H(T)=1. In the plane, all finite circuits in the triangle-adjacency graph are generated by these vertex circuits: this is the usual cellular duality statement, since the dual triangulation is a locally finite cell decomposition of a simply connected surface. Thus the propagated scales are path independent. The resulting intrinsic Euclidean triangle surface has angle2π at every vertex. This proves consistent edge lengths and a locally Euclidean metric; it does not assert metric completeness or an injective global developing map. No argument here identifies these auxiliary dual lengths with primal side lengths or distances between chosen tile centers.

For a fixed prototile and an oriented native side e, the factor `sin(end angle)/sin(start angle)` lies in a finite exact set, with reversal giving its reciprocal. Thus H is a finite native-side multiplicative holonomy diagnostic. Taking logarithms makes H=1 a linear closed-circuit condition in these fixed real constants. It is stronger information than the angular curvature equation, but a tiling has not been shown to satisfy it.

In the corrected narrow `(90°,120°]` gate all occurring angles are120°, so every factor is1 and H is identically1. The auxiliary dual metric then has equilateral triangles and recovers only honeycomb combinatorics. It carries no information about the two species or the nonuniform primal side lengths. This explains exactly why this diagnostic produces no additional return invariant there.

## Failed bridge and stopping point

No proof was found that primal geometric realizability forces H(T)=1 in the broad obtuse case; no exact two-obtuse-shape plane counterexample to that proposed implication was constructed in this cycle. The existing native polygon closure identities do not imply the displayed neighbor-angle product. Assuming it would add an unproved hypothesis. Even establishing H=1 everywhere would produce a flat auxiliary metric, not a periodic species field or periodic original embedding.

The precise missing global step for this proposed mechanism is: derive from the two unmarked polygon shapes a constraint on the realizable species/native-edge field on this dual surface that yields a repeating embedded strip or a compact translation quotient, with compatible original edge displacement and reflection holonomy. Neither zero dual angular curvature nor the multiplicative sine condition supplies that constraint. This is theorem-strength without an active mechanism. Stop this particular cycle under protocol §5; do not extend it into a torus-size search, repackage the certain-transition criterion, or use a star-density ledger as a periodicity proof.

A justified restart would require either an exact obtuse two-shape tiling violating H=1 (to kill automatic flat-dual claims), or a new intrinsic restriction on the two-shape field beyond these angle/holonomy equations.
