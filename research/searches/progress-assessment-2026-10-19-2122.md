## Direction 2 — a new nonnegative charge test on the mixed-Q7 incidence cone

**Completed work is not a new assignment.** The parameter-wide BB=CC/closure obstruction now excludes ABBBCCC and ABBCBCC, with independent Sol PASS in `research/reviews/heptagon-mixed-metric-hostile-sol-2026-10-19.md`. **New object and precise proposed mechanism.** Test whether native incidence and the BC matching imply a nonnegative conservation law that was absent from the old seam atlas. Work in a defect-free tiling, justified at alpha120 by the reviewed purification. Each P has d=0,1 or2 PP sides: PP sides cannot be adjacent because a PPA vertex has exactly one PP seam. Each Q has one A corner, hence exactly one associated PP edge; each such edge has two A endpoints. Consequently p_R=q_R+O(R), the sum of PP degrees is q_R+O(R), and if n_d counts P with degree d then

    n_0 − n_2 = O(R).

This identity does **not** make either n_0 or n_2 sparse. It is precisely why simply copying octagon saturation fails. The desired new certificate is a nonnegative native-incidence combination giving

    n_0 + n_2 = O(R),

possibly together with elimination of specified reflected BB/CC seam states. If obtained, translated compactness would give a tiling in which every P belongs to a PP dimer. That would be a new structural reduction for these words, not itself a periodicity proof.

**Exact finite gate.** Enumerate complete native side/corner incidence types for one P and one Q, with the actual cyclic word, independent reflections and the PPA/PBC endpoint rules. Keep Q's BB, CC and BC sides distinct; BB meets CC and BC meets BC with complementary endpoint labels. Include enough flags to identify which two P incidences refer to the same PP edge and which Q contributes its A endpoint. This is a symbolic incidence domain, not a larger geometric neighborhood or a sampled alpha. Give its precise completeness argument before using it. Form the rational incidence-flow cone with nonnegative type counts, side matching, corner matching, and normalized p=q=1. Separate the four words and retain every permitted parity. Extra constraints require an actual proof from native incidence; do not assume an embedded strip, a periodic quotient or a chosen chirality.

A new valid inequality forcing both terms to zero must be written as a checkable linear combination of named conservation laws and nonnegative counts. If the cone is unbounded in auxiliary variables, use a homogeneous normalization covering all nonzero flows; do not rely on a floating-point LP conclusion. Exact rational witnesses can be independently checked without trusting the solver.
