# Resumed pentagon/higher-partner metric resource mechanism

Date:2026-10-13. Status: both analytic obstructions independently reviewed
VALID; unrestricted objective unresolved. No novelty claim.

[Self-contained extracted theorem and proofs](../lemmas/adjacent-low-octagon-alpha-alpha-length-obstruction.md)
show that for an equiangular108 pentagon paired with an octagon having two
adjacent108 corners and six144 corners, actual tileability requires the
108–108 side length to equal a144–144 Q side length. This strengthens the
initial unique private-side obstruction. Other lengths may repeat and the
108–108 length may occur on P or108–144 Q sides. Reflections, unrestricted
contacts, sparse/omitted species and arbitrary metrics are included.

The mechanism uses all-obtuse full-side/trivalent forcing, native-corner
balance, nonnegative contact deficits and a bounded-multiplicity association
of every Q to a defective tile. No parameter sweep or computational proof
component was used. The source-shaped tangential Q fixture has side
multiplicities1,2,5 and violates the necessary equality. A regular P of
side2tan18 shares Q's five144–144 sides but cannot rescue plane tilability.
