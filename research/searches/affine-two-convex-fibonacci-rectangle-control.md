# Exact anisotropic two-rectangle control

Status: internally proved control. Not an aperiodic pair.

Let phi=(1+sqrt(5))/2, A=[0,1]x[0,1], B=[0,phi]x[0,1], and M=diag(phi,2). These are two distinct unmarked convex congruence classes, and M is an expanding nonsimilarity with no scalar power.

MA is exactly two B tiles stacked vertically: B and B+(0,1). MB is exactly two rows, each consisting of B followed by A: B+(0,j), A+(phi,j), j=0,1. The widths sum to phi+1=phi^2 and heights sum to 2. Hence both inflated parents are covered exactly with disjoint interiors; all placements have orthogonal part I. Iteration is legal. Each individual rectangle also tiles periodically by its edge-vector lattice, decisively killing geometry-only aperiodicity.

For a stronger quantifier control the pair admits an explicit tiling with no translation symmetry. Put beta=1/phi^2 and x_n=n+(phi-1)floor(n beta) for n in Z. The successive intervals have lengths 1 or phi and cover R. In strip j<=y<=j+1 use these intervals shifted horizontally by s_j=j^2. Every resulting tile is A or B and the strips cover the whole plane.

The endpoint set F={x_n} has no nonzero translation symmetry: an order-preserving translation induces an index shift m on its bi-infinite ordered endpoints. If m is nonzero, the gap sequence floor((n+1)beta)-floor(n beta) would be periodic with period |m|; its limiting mean beta is irrational, whereas a periodic binary sequence has rational mean. If m=0 the translation is zero.

Any tiling translation (u,v) has v=k in Z because tile lower-edge heights are integers. In corresponding strip interiors it must satisfy F+s_j+u=F+s_(j+k). The absence of periods of F implies u=s_(j+k)-s_j=2jk+k^2 for every j, forcing k=0 and then u=0. Thus one admitted tiling is aperiodic while other admitted tilings are periodic. This construction explains exactly why a self-affine covering or even an explicit nonperiodic covering is insufficient for the target.

No numerical search or finite enumeration supports these claims. They follow from phi^2=phi+1 and elementary irrational rotation counting. The nonperiodic strip tiling is not asserted to be generated by the displayed substitution. Independent review of the affine obstruction is separate.
