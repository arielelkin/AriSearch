# Prior-art boundary for the generic boundary-module obstruction

Search date: 2026-10-10. Purpose: compare the elementary boundary
length-module and eigenvalue mechanism in the generic U/V homothetic
subdivision obstruction with primary substitution-tiling literature.
No universal novelty claim is made.

Primary source inspected:

Richard Kenyon, *The Construction of Self-Similar Tilings*,
[arXiv:math/9505210v1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/9505210v1),
1995 preprint of the 1996 Geometric and Functional Analysis paper.
Section3.1 on printed page5 defines the nonnegative subdivision matrix
and obtains its area eigenvalue from tile areas. Thus the general
subdivision-matrix/area-eigenvector mechanism is established primary
literature and is not a new principle here.

Scope matters: the definition on printed pages1–3 uses finitely many
translation classes and quasiperiodicity. The source explicitly discusses
that replacing translation equivalence by arbitrary isometries yields a
broader notion. Its Perron expansion results therefore were not imported
to justify the present arbitrary-isometry subdivision obstruction or the
monohedral compactness limit. The present proof derives its specific
two-dimensional boundary matrix directly from the two independent length
parameters and uses only elementary field algebra and positivity.

A search result also located Kenyon's *Tilings of convex polygons*,
Annales de l'Institut Fourier, [primary catalog page](https://numdam.org/articles/10.5802/aif.1586/).
Opening that page timed out in this audit. Its contents were not available
and no theorem from it was used or asserted. It remains a concrete source
to inspect if a broader algebraic-dissection mechanism is pursued.
