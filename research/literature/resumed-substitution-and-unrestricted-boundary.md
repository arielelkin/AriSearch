## Primary pages examined

[Zhu, Wang, Yan, Hexagonal Tiling of the Plane, arXiv:2508.01404v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.01404v2),
version dated2026-04-28. Introduction and Main Theorem inspected.
The theorem concerns congruent hexagons with at least three tiles at every
vertex; convex hexagons satisfy the hypothesis. It gives the three
Reinhardt families. This is a monohedral result; it does not classify
arbitrary two-shape hexagon tilings. Existing repository density/hexagon
lemmas already use this source. No new use to establish pair periodicity.

[Rao, Exhaustive search of convex pentagons which tile the plane,
arXiv:1708.00274v1](https://arxiv.org/html/1708.00274v1), primary HTML
reopened. This is monohedral computer-assisted classification prior art.
The earlier repository records retain its validation boundary; no new
independent audit of the complete computation was performed here, and no
mixed-pair theorem is inferred from it.

[Kenyon, Tilings of convex polygons, Annales de l'Institut Fourier47(1997),
929–944, DOI10.5802/aif.1586](https://numdam.org/articles/10.5802/aif.1586/),
primary journal metadata and author abstract inspected. It addresses finite
partitions with rational length-ratio or coordinate-rational polygons and
associated algebraic conditions. The full proof was not inspected, so no
theorem is imported. It is distinct from shape-only forcing by exactly two
fixed congruence classes; unrestricted mixing of rational tiles must not be
silently equated with this target.
