Web searches: `pinwheel tiling substitution convex pentagons dual tiling vertices`; `pinwheel tiling right triangles vertex configurations valence`. Searches lead to the following primary sources:

* Charles Radin, The pinwheel tilings of the plane, Annals of Mathematics139 (1994),661–702; [author-hosted paper](https://web.ma.utexas.edu/users/radin/papers/pinwheel.pdf). The PDF is accessible but has no machine-readable text in the web tool. The decorated matching construction is not treated as unmarked shape-only forcing.
* Natalie Priebe Frank and Michael F. Whittaker, [A fractal version of the pinwheel tiling](https://arxiv.org/abs/1001.2203), arXiv1001.2203v3, Mathematical Intelligencer33:2 (2011),7–17. The abstract explicitly describes thirteen basic prototiles with fractal boundaries and mutual local derivability with the pinwheel tiling space. These are outside convex polygon geometry and are not a two-convex construction.
* Natalie Priebe Frank, Samuel B. G. Webster and Michael F. Whittaker, [Fractal dual substitution tilings](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.4708), arXiv1410.4708v1, Journal of Fractal Geometry3 (2016),265–317. The abstract describes graph iterated-function constructions, fractal boundaries, and mutual local derivability of derived tiling spaces. It does not establish convex geometric forcing for two unmarked polygons.
