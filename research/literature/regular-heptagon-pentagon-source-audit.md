# Prior-art audit for the regular-heptagon/pentagon mechanism

**Status:** limited primary-source boundary; the positive tiling is already
illustrated in a 2003 publisher source. No novelty claim is made for either
the periodic witness or the present restricted classification.

query families:

Search results included general education pages, monohedral pentagon papers,
polygon packings, and hyperbolic tilings. Those are not imported as a proof
of the two-shape unrestricted classification. The sole closest positive
primary artifact actually inspected is the publisher source below.

## Primary source examined

Texas Instruments, *Geometric Investigations on the Voyage 200 with Cabri*,
2003, Teacher's Guide: Tessellations and Tile Patterns, printed page26,
Figure6 (PDF page index1):

[Publisher PDF](https://education.ti.com/~/media/461F7962E9CF4A10998FDE6F9EF1E1F9).
The printed page explicitly asserts a plane tessellation using a regular
heptagon and a pentagon, and shows its repeating arrangement. The actual
page was rendered from the publisher PDF and visually inspected. Its
pentagon/heptagon pattern is consistent with the present four-motif witness;
that congruence is an inference from the diagram, not an exact coordinate
identity supplied by the source. The source gives no exact angle list,
coordinates, uniqueness theorem, or classification of all obtuse partners.

This is enough to rule out treating mere existence of a periodic regular-
heptagon/pentagon arrangement as new. The independent exact certificate in
[the current classification](../lemmas/all-obtuse-pentagon-regular-heptagon-classification.md)
remains useful for replay, and its shape-determination argument must stand
on its own. The source is not used as a mathematical dependency for coverage
or for the classification.

## Boundary and remaining uncertainty

Within the listed searches and examined primary artifact, no complete
classification of every strictly obtuse pentagon partner of a regular
heptagon was identified. This is a limited search outcome, not a priority
assertion or evidence of universal novelty. A broader review of tiling
monographs and their bibliographies would be required before claiming that
restricted classification as new. No result about general convex pairs,
acute/right-corner pentagons, or nonregular heptagons is inferred.
