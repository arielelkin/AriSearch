# Primary-source boundary for the bounded consolidation cycle

Date: 2026-10-11. This records a narrow source check, not exhaustive coverage
or a priority claim. Searches used web search followed by direct primary opens.

Exact queries:

- `"convex" "two prototiles" aperiodic hexagon pentagon`
- `"Cairo" "two" pentagons tiling periodic non edge to edge`
- `"convex hexagons" "tiling" Zhu Wang Yan classification`

Primary sources examined:

- [Zhu–Wang–Yan, Hexagonal Tiling of the Plane, arXiv:2508.01404v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2508.01404v2), introduction, H1/H2/H3 definitions and Main Theorem. The text is v2 dated 28 April 2026. Its main theorem classifies a single congruence class with straight hexagonal edges under the at-least-three-tiles-at-a-vertex assumption, satisfied by convex hexagons. H1 and H2 require an equal opposite side pair; H3 requires three adjacent equal pairs. This confirms the imported binary `pppqqq`, p!=q monohedral exclusion. It does not classify two hexagon species. Its two-corona geometric elimination is a possible method comparison, not a theorem imported for mixed pairs.
- [Sugimoto, Convex Polygons for Aperiodic Tiling, arXiv:1602.06372v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.06372), abstract. This is the edge-to-edge monohedral periodic-escape statement, not the unrestricted two-shape classification sought here. No additional theorem is imported from its abstract.
- [Perimeter-minimizing pentagonal tilings, Involve 7(4), 2014](https://msp.org/involve/2014/7-4/involve-v7-n4-p02-s.pdf), primary PDF opened. Perimeter minimization and special Cairo/prismatic metrics are a comparison domain; no theorem from this source is used to assert arbitrary-metric Cairo necessity. A direct open is not a complete substantive audit.

Secondary search results were not accepted as mathematical evidence. These
queries did not establish a matching unrestricted theorem for the remaining
Cairo contact targets or both-homogeneous two-species hexagons. The search
boundary is limited to these queries and sections; failure to find a matching
result is not evidence of novelty. No publication-priority claim is made for
any new synthesis, carrier, count obstruction or proposed alternative mechanism.
