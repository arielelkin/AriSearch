   dated2026-04-28. Introduction, Main Theorem and geometric Lemmas3-4 were
   inspected. The theorem classifies congruent hexagon plane tilings when

   recomposition is aperiodic and mutually locally derivable with Penrose.
   This remains an established benchmark; the repository source proof is
   verification/strengthening, not a new three-tile discovery.
3. [Sugimoto, Aperiodic sets of three types of convex polygons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.00534),
   primary abstract record. Three convex pentagon types are obtained from
   a chiral source. This refresh does not establish that its reflection
   convention matches the present unrestricted two-shape objective.
