## Additional resumption search: contact mechanisms

Executed 2026-10-10 through web search and direct primary-source opens. Queries:

* `two convex polygons aperiodic tiling unmarked 2026`
* `aperiodic convex hexagons two tiles equiangular`
* `convex pentagon tiling partial edge contacts periodicity two prototiles`

Primary records examined:

* [Sugimoto, arXiv:2609.09603v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.09603v1),
  submitted 9 September 2026. Abstract, introduction, and the discussion
  following Section 2.3 concern concave pairs obtained by grouping a Type 5
  pentagon tiling according to hat/turtle clusters. The author explicitly
  distinguishes producing nonperiodic tilings from proving the resulting
  pair aperiodic. These concave cluster shapes do not meet our convex target.
  This is a current nearby construction, not an imported solution.
* [Sugimoto, arXiv:1508.01864v4](https://arxiv.org/html/1508.01864v4),
  revised 10 July 2017. The abstract's monotile exclusion allows an
  edge-to-edge restriction. It does not classify two unmarked polygons
  with unrestricted partial contacts. No new theorem from it is used here.
* [Socolar--Taylor, arXiv:1003.4279v2](https://arxiv.org/html/1003.4279v2),
  revised 9 September 2010. Its abstract identifies markings on the planar
  hexagon, and disconnected planar or three-dimensional alternatives for
  shape enforcement. Those conventions do not meet our target.

  is certified from that source.
