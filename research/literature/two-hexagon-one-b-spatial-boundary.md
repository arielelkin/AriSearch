# Literature boundary for the one-B spatial generalization

Search date2026-10-12. This is a bounded primary-source refresh, not a priority
claim or a comprehensive literature review. It supplements the broader
[corrected convex-aperiodicity boundary](2026-10-11-convex-aperiodic-boundary.md).

Exact queries used with source filters:

- `"convex hexagons" "aperiodic" tilings` (arxiv.org).
- `"two convex" "aperiodic" tiles` (arxiv.org,cambridge.org,link.springer.com).

Primary records read directly:

1. [Teruhisa Sugimoto, Convex Polygons for Aperiodic Tiling](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.06372),
   submitted2016,revised2017. Its abstract concerns periodic escape for an
   edge-to-edge monohedral convex polygon. It is not the present two-shape,
   unrestricted-contact classification; no stronger pair theorem is imported.
2. [Vivian Olsiewski Healey, A Family of Recompositions of the Penrose Aperiodic
   Protoset and Its Dynamic Properties](https://arxiv.org/abs/0912.3814),
   submitted2009. Its abstract asserts a three-prototile unmarked Ammann
   recomposition result and a Penrose-linked dynamical description. The
   existing repository record analyzes its conventions more closely. It
   remains relevant to construction, not a two-hexagon impossibility result.
3. [Jeong-Yup Lee and Robert V.Moody, Taylor-Socolar hexagonal tilings as model
   sets](https://arxiv.org/abs/1207.6237),submitted2012, primary abstract.
   The tilings use regular hexagons with two colors and matching/substitution
   structure. The unmarked geometric hexagon alone admits periodic tilings,
   so colored parity dynamics do not supply the requested shape-only forcing.
4. [Mehmet Koca,Nazife Ozdes Koca,Ramazan Koc, Quasi Regular Polyhedra and Their
   Duals with Coxeter Symmetries Represented by Quaternions I](https://arxiv.org/abs/1006.2434),
   submitted2010, primary abstract. It describes aperiodic arrangements using
   isogonal polygons with regular polygons. Such an arrangement is not an
   assertion that its whole unmarked protoset forces aperiodicity. If the
   protoset includes a regular hexagon, its monohedral periodic escape already
   rules out the target. This is a mathematical scope inference, not a
   criticism of the paper's stated arrangement result.

No primary source located in these bounded searches supplies the particular
full unique-one-B-star family theorem proved in this run. Abstract/metadata
search alone does not establish novelty. Full-text citation chains and wider
searches remain necessary before any publication or priority claim. The
finite-function strip argument is elementary; it is not advertised as a new
general symbolic-dynamics theorem. Determinism from two predecessor tiles is
different from the single-successor relation used here and is not excluded
by that argument.
