## Interpretation and open identification gates

The preceding paragraph is a source summary. The following are our audit
inferences, with scope limits:

* The paper is a close prior-art lead for a dodecagon/deformed-pentagon
  construction; it prevents treating the general visual idea as new.
* The exact role of the caption's fragments has not been resolved. They
  could indicate additional internal shapes or clipped displayed cells;
  the caption alone does not decide the full-plane protoset count.
* The specified density-wave selection is extra generator information.
  An example with quasicrystalline order does not by itself show that all
  unrestricted tilings of its unmarked shapes exclude translation periods.
  No such shape-only theorem was identified in the inspected construction.
* We have not reconstructed the source pentagon's exact coordinates,
  convexity, acute/right/obtuse boundary, or lengths relative to Q. Thus
  we neither identify it with our family nor reject it from that family.
* If that source pentagon is a genuine strictly obtuse member of our
  classified family, the independent periodic escape applies to its shape;
  a quasicrystalline example remains logically consistent with that escape.

The [local classification and periodic-family proof](../lemmas/all-obtuse-pentagon-regular-dodecagon-classification.md)
was developed independently of this paper. Its correctness is checked by
mathematical review, not by the absence of a literature match. Its novelty

[large-regular-polygon obstruction](../lemmas/all-obtuse-pentagon-regular-large-polygon-obstruction.md)
