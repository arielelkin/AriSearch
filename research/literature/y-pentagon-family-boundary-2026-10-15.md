priority, and not a mathematical solution to the two-convex-polygon problem.

Primary arXiv landing pages freshly opened:

1. [Sugimoto, Convex Polygons for Aperiodic Tiling](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.06372),
   v2, 2017-07-17. Its abstract concerns an edge-to-edge **monohedral**
   periodicity theorem. That statement cannot decide mixed unrestricted
   tilings of the present two pentagons.
2. [Sugimoto, Aperiodic sets of three types of convex polygons](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.00534),
   v5, 2025-01-15. The landing abstract describes three types obtained from
   a chiral source. It does not state the present Y-family obstruction.
   The new check did not repeat the earlier full-paper reflection audit.
3. [Zhu, Wang, Yan, Hexagonal Tiling of the Plane](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.01404),
   v2, 2026-04-28. Its abstract classifies monohedral hexagon tilers. It is
   neither a mixed-pentagon theorem nor an input to the new proof.
4. [Sugimoto, Tile sets consisting of two types of concave polygons...](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.09603),
   v1, 2026-09-09. The abstract concerns concave polygons derived from
   Type5 pentagon constructions, outside the convex-pair target.

Only these primary landing-page statements were freshly checked; no new
full-text mathematical theorem is imported. Existing literature records
retain their separate full-text coverage and qualifications.

## Relationship to previous repository work

The exact t=2 shapes were invented and analyzed in the 2026-10-14
interface-first record. Their omission exclusions, full-edge obstruction,
and full-BB-mate theorem were already retained and independently reviewed.
Those results left unrestricted mixed existence open. The new result supplies
an unrestricted contradiction using rigid GG endpoints and an RA endpoint
guard. It then proves a self-contained abstract obstruction and an infinite
rational-coordinate parameter family, rather than extrapolating a numerical
experiment. No geometric star enumeration or prior source in the inspected
boundary was found to state this particular family theorem.

## Permitted novelty statement

The unrestricted Y-pair obstruction and its displayed rational parameter
family are **new relative to the repository and the inspected query/source
boundary**. They are narrow untileability theorems; elementary endpoint
arguments and local angle atlases are established techniques. We do not
claim that no equivalent family exclusion exists in the wider literature.
Neither a globally aperiodic convex pair nor a general impossibility theorem
has been obtained.
