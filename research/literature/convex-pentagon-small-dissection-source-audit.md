# Source boundary for the small convex pentagon dissection obstruction

Search date: 2026-10-10. Purpose: attack the proposed claim that a genuine
convex pentagon cannot be finitely partitioned into two through five
genuine convex pentagons, even with partial seams. The theorem's proof
is elementary and self-contained; this search does not supply a missing
geometric premise or establish universal priority.

Primary source examined:

Dirk Frettlöh and Christian Richter, *Incongruent equipartitions of the
plane*, [arXiv:1905.08144v2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.08144v2),
European Journal of Combinatorics87 (2020), article103129. Section1.2,
printed pages2–3 and Figure1, gives a convex pentagon partitioned into
six convex pentagons, with continuous choices preserving their areas.
This is a six-piece construction, not a counterexample with fewer pieces.
It places the six-piece mechanism firmly inside existing primary literature.

Also examined:

Dirk Frettlöh and Christian Richter, *Fair partitions of the plane into
incongruent pentagons*, [journal primary PDF](https://cdm.ucalgary.ca/article/download/74719/58185/265014),
2025. Its convex-pentagon construction subdivides perturbed hexagons.
It does not provide a convex-pentagon parent with two through five convex
pentagon children, nor was the claimed lower bound located there.

Search results also suggested original Wolfram demonstrations titled
*Dissection of a Regular Pentagon into Three Pentagons* and *Another
Hinged Dissection of One Pentagon to Five Pentagons*. Their pages required
JavaScript and returned no inspectable geometric data through the browser.
Titles alone do not certify a simultaneous convex partition, so neither
was used as mathematical evidence. A future source audit can inspect
the underlying demonstration geometry if priority or a suspected
counterexample makes that worthwhile.

Boundary: web search, the two named primary papers, and attempted access
to those original demonstration pages. No complete review of polygon
dissection literature or historical priority is claimed. The bounded
failure to locate an earlier lower-bound statement is not evidence that
the theorem is new. The general theorem's independent mathematical audit
is in [the review](../reviews/convex-pentagon-small-dissection-review.md).
